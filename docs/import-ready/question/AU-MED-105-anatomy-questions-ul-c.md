## title
Deep muscles of the posterior compartment of the forearm

## question
All of the followings are deep muscles of posterior compartment of forearm EXCEPT:

## subject
msk

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
D

## answer_a
Abductor pollicis longus.

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Abductor pollicis longus is one of the deep-layer thumb muscles.

## answer_b
Extensor pollicis longus.

## explanation_b
Incorrect. Extensor pollicis longus is one of the deep-layer thumb muscles.

## answer_c
Extensor pollicis brevis.

## explanation_c
Incorrect. Extensor pollicis brevis is one of the deep-layer thumb muscles.

## answer_d
Extensor carpi radialis longus.

## explanation_d
Correct. The posterior compartment of the forearm has a superficial layer (brachioradialis, extensor carpi radialis longus and brevis, extensor digitorum, extensor digiti minimi, extensor carpi ulnaris, anconeus) and a deep layer (supinator, abductor pollicis longus, extensor pollicis brevis, extensor pollicis longus, extensor indicis). Extensor carpi radialis longus belongs to the superficial layer, arising from the lateral supracondylar ridge of the humerus above the elbow, which is why it is the exception here. The three thumb muscles and extensor indicis are the deep layer's own group, all supplied by the posterior interosseous nerve and all emerging to cross the wrist extensors obliquely.

## title
Muscle supplied by the radial nerve

## question
Which of the following muscles is supplied by the radial nerve?

## subject
msk

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
A

## answer_a
Extensor carpi radialis longus

## explanation_a
Correct. Extensor carpi radialis longus arises from the lateral supracondylar ridge and is supplied by the main trunk of the radial nerve before it divides into its superficial and deep (posterior interosseous) branches, making it one of the few forearm extensors supplied by the radial nerve proper rather than by the posterior interosseous nerve. Every other posterior-compartment muscle distal to it takes the posterior interosseous branch instead. This makes extensor carpi radialis longus a useful landmark: its power is preserved even in some posterior interosseous nerve lesions that paralyse the rest of the extensors.

## answer_b
Abductor pollicis longus

## explanation_b
Incorrect. Abductor pollicis longus is a deep extensor supplied by the posterior interosseous nerve.

## answer_c
Flexor pollicis longus

## explanation_c
Incorrect. Flexor pollicis longus is a flexor-compartment muscle supplied by the anterior interosseous nerve.

## answer_d
Flexor carpi radialis

## explanation_d
Incorrect. Flexor carpi radialis is a flexor-compartment muscle supplied by the median nerve.

The following structures passes deep to the flexor retinaculum at wrist, EXCEPT:

## explanation_b
Correct. Muscle supplied by the posterior interosseous nerve

## question
Which muscle is supplied by the posterior interosseous nerve?

## subject

## status

## owner

## vignette

Brachioradialis.

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Brachioradialis is supplied by the radial nerve trunk, proximal to the posterior interosseous branch.

## answer_b
Extensor carpi ulnaris.

## explanation_b

Extensor carpi ulnaris is one of the deep extensor-compartment muscles supplied by the posterior interosseous nerve, the deep terminal branch of the radial nerve given off after it pierces supinator. Brachialis
