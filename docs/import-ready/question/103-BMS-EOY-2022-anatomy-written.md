## question
Answer the five parts below about this patient: the likely diagnosis, what is meant by the femoral ring, what is meant by the femoral canal, why the condition is more common in females, and its common complication.

Part (c)'s concept is the femoral sheath concept rather than the femoral hernia one, since it asks for the femoral canal's own structural definition, which that concept, not this one, states directly.
contextual_concept_ids names the femoral-triangle-contents concept (the canal is one of the sheath's own three compartments, itself one of the triangle's four contents) and the abnormal-obturator-artery concept (an operative hazard in a strangulated femoral hernia, named in this record's own article but not asked about directly here).
