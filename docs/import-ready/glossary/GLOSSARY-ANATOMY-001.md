# Item

## term
Anatomical position

## ar

## category
Directional & anatomy

## def
The agreed starting posture — standing, facing forward, palms turned to the front — that every direction word is measured from.

## definition_ar
الوضع المتفق عليه للجسم: واقفًا وناظرًا للأمام وراحتا اليدين إلى الأمام، وتُقاس منه كل كلمات الاتجاه.

## example
Say the thumb is lateral only because you are describing the anatomical position.
