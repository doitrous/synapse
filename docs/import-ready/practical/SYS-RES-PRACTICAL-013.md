## candidate_instructions
Measure this patient's peak expiratory flow and record the result. Explain the manoeuvre to them as you go.
## mark_scheme
Preparation (5): Washes hands and introduces self, confirming patient identity
Preparation (5): Explains the purpose of the measurement in plain language and gains consent
Preparation (5): Checks the meter is clean, fits a mouthpiece, and sets the pointer to zero
Positioning (3): Asks the patient to stand, or to sit upright if standing is not possible
Positioning (3): Ensures the patient holds the meter level and keeps fingers clear of the scale
The manoeuvre (7): Instructs a full inspiration to total lung capacity before the blow
The manoeuvre (7): Instructs a tight seal around the mouthpiece with the lips
The manoeuvre (7): Instructs a single short, maximal, forceful blow rather than a prolonged one
Repetition (3): Repeats the measurement three times, allowing recovery between attempts
Recording (2): Records the highest of the three readings, not the mean
Recording (2): Compares the result with the patient's own best or with a predicted value for age, sex and height
