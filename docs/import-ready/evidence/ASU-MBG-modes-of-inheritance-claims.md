## subject
Autosomal dominant inheritance

## predicate
produces

## object
a vertical pedigree pattern — an affected individual in every generation, either sex equally affected, and one affected parent sufficient to transmit the trait — though the phenotype's severity can still vary between individuals carrying the identical mutation (variable expressivity), and each pregnancy of an affected-by-unaffected mating carries an independent 50% risk regardless of prior children's status

## display_text

## concept_id
CON-FND-592065F09E7EC8

## subject
X-linked recessive inheritance

## predicate

## object
males almost exclusively, because a male has only one X chromosome and any recessive allele on it is unopposed: a carrier mother transmits the disease allele to 50% of her sons (who are affected) and 50% of her daughters (who become carriers), while an affected father's daughters are all obligate carriers and none of his sons are affected — Duchenne muscular dystrophy and haemophilia are the classic examples
