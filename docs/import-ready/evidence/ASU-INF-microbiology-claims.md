## object
The term "hospital-acquired infection" (nosocomial infection) is best defined as an infection acquired in a healthcare setting more than 48 hours after admission, and which was not already incubating (present but not yet symptomatic) at the time of admission -- distinguishing it from a community-acquired infection or an infection that was already incubating before the patient arrived at the healthcare facility.

The ASU Year 1 infection microbiology MCQ bank teaches that the term "hospital-acquired infection" (nosocomial infection) is best defined as an infection acquired in a healthcare setting more than 48 hours after admission, and which was not already incubating (present but not yet symptomatic) at the time of admission -- distinguishing it from a community-acquired infection or an infection that was already incubating before the patient arrived at the healthcare facility.
