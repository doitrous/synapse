## title
Cell types among the small intestinal epithelium

## question
Among the enterocytes ,Which types of cells can be found :

## subject
gi

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
C

## answer_a
M-cells

## explanation_a
Incorrect. M cells are specialised, restricted to the epithelium over Peyer's patches for antigen sampling; the bank's printed key marks goblet cells, not M cells alone, as the answer.

## answer_b
Entroendocrine cells

## explanation_b
Incorrect. Enteroendocrine cells are present but scattered and sparse; the bank's printed key marks goblet cells, not enteroendocrine cells alone, as the answer.

## answer_c
Goblet cells

## explanation_c
Correct. The small-intestinal surface epithelium includes mucus-secreting goblet cells interspersed with absorptive cells all along its length, increasing in number distally toward the ileum — the most consistently abundant additional cell type in this population.

A and B

## explanation_d
Incorrect, since the printed key marks goblet cells (C) specifically rather than the combination of M cells and enteroendocrine cells.

## id
QST-AUMED202-GITFINAL-HISTOLOGY-GITFINAL-Q14

## title
Opening of the intestinal gland

## question
The opening of the intestinal gland is :

## subject
gi

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
B

## answer_a
In the brush border

## explanation_a
Incorrect. The brush border is the microvillous absorptive surface of the villi themselves, not where intestinal glands open.

## answer_b
Between the base of the villi

## explanation_b
Correct. Intestinal glands (crypts of Lieberkuhn) are simple tubular glands that open onto the mucosal surface between the bases of adjacent villi, housing at their own base the stem-cell niche (with Paneth cells) that repopulates crypt and villus epithelium.

## answer_c
Superficial part of the villi

## explanation_c
Incorrect. The superficial part of the villi is absorptive surface, not the site of intestinal gland openings.

## answer_d
NONE OF THE ABOVE

## explanation_d
Incorrect, since option B (between the base of the villi) correctly describes the intestinal gland's opening.

## title
Antibody class secreted by Peyer's patches

## question
Which type of antibodies that are secreted by peyer's patches

## subject
gi

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
C

## answer_a
IgM

## explanation_a
Incorrect. IgM is the first antibody class produced in a primary immune response generally, but it is not the class secreted by Peyer's patches' mature mucosal plasma cells.

## answer_b
IgG

## explanation_b
Incorrect. IgG dominates systemic humoral immunity, not the mucosal secretions produced by Peyer's patches.

## answer_c
IgA

## explanation_c
Correct. Peyer's patches' germinal-centre B cells differentiate into IgA-secreting plasma cells; the secreted dimeric IgA is transcytosed across the overlying epithelium into the gut lumen, neutralising luminal antigens without triggering inflammation.

## answer_d
B and C

## explanation_d
Incorrect, since the bank's printed key marks IgA (C) alone, not the combination of IgG and IgA.
