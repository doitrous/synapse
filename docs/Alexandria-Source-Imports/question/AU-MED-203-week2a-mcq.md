## title
Embryological origin of the smooth contour of the neck

## question
Embryological origin of smooth contour of the neck is:

## subject
neuro

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
C

## answer_a
4th pouch

## explanation_a
Incorrect. The fourth pharyngeal pouch contributes the superior parathyroid glands and ultimobranchial body, unrelated to the smoothing of the neck's external contour.

## answer_b
First pharyngeal cleft

## explanation_b
Incorrect. The first pharyngeal cleft persists as the external acoustic meatus; it is not the structure that smooths the neck's overall contour.

## answer_c
Operculum

## explanation_c
Correct. As the second arch overgrows the third and fourth arches, its caudal margin (the operculum) grows down over the transient depression (the cervical sinus of His) formed between them, fusing at the neck's lower part and obliterating the sinus. This active overgrowth by the operculum is what produces the neck's smooth external contour. In short: identify the operculum as the embryological structure responsible for the smooth contour of the neck.

## answer_d
Cervical sinus

## explanation_d
Incorrect. The cervical sinus itself is the transient DEPRESSION being obliterated, not the active structure that produces the smoothing; that role belongs to the operculum's overgrowth.

The pharyngeal arch supplied by the facial nerve

Which of the following arches is supplied by the facial nerve?

## subject
neuro

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

A

## answer_a
2nd arch

## explanation_a
Correct. The second (hyoid) pharyngeal arch is the facial nerve's own arch, supplying all of its derivatives, the muscles of facial expression, stapedius, stylohyoid and the posterior belly of digastric. In short: identify the second pharyngeal arch as the arch supplied by the facial nerve.

3rd arch

## explanation_b
Incorrect. The third arch is supplied by the glossopharyngeal nerve, not the facial nerve.

4th arch

## explanation_c
Incorrect. The fourth arch is supplied by the superior laryngeal branch of the vagus nerve, not the facial nerve.

None of the above

Incorrect. Option A correctly names the second arch, so 'none of the above' is wrong.
