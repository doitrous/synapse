## title
Location of Brunner's glands

## question
In which part of small intestine ,we can see brunner's glands :

## subject
gi

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
B

## answer_a
The third part of duodenum

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Brunner's glands are concentrated in the submucosa of the first part of the duodenum, not the third part.

## answer_b
The duodenal cap

## explanation_b
Correct. Brunner's glands are compound tubuloalveolar glands located in the submucosa of the first part of the duodenum (the duodenal cap), secreting alkaline mucus that neutralises acidic chyme as it enters from the stomach.

## answer_c
The jejunum

## explanation_c
Incorrect. Brunner's glands are a duodenal, not jejunal, structure; the jejunum lacks them.

## answer_d
The ileum

## explanation_d
Incorrect. Brunner's glands are absent from the ileum, which is instead characterised by abundant Peyer's patches.

## title
Main site of intestinal digestion

## question
The main site of the intestinal digestion is :

## subject
gi

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
D

## answer_a
In the cells and cytoplasm

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Intracellular cytoplasm hydrolyses only some di- and tripeptides; it is not the main overall site of intestinal digestion.

## answer_b
In the lumen of small intestine

## explanation_b
Incorrect. The lumen is where pancreatic enzymes carry out the initial, bulk breakdown of a meal, but the final, defining digestive steps happen at the brush border membrane itself.

## answer_c
In the crypts of lieberkuhn

## explanation_c
Incorrect. The crypts of Lieberkuhn are secretory and regenerative (stem-cell) structures, not the main site of digestion.

## answer_d
In the brush border

## explanation_d
Correct. Membrane (contact) digestion at the enterocyte's brush border — where enzymes such as maltase, sucrase, lactase and peptidases complete disaccharide and oligopeptide hydrolysis immediately before absorption — is the main site of intestinal digestion.

## title
False statement about intestinal digestion (trehalase)

## question
Which of the following sentences are not true:

## subject
gi

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
A

## answer_a
Trehalase enzyme hydrolyzes trehalose into one glucose molecule

## explanation_a
Correct — this is the FALSE statement. Trehalose is a disaccharide of two glucose units; the brush-border enzyme trehalase hydrolyses it into two free glucose molecules, not one.

## answer_b
The life span of an intestinal mucosal cell is 3-5 days

## explanation_b
Incorrect as an answer to 'not true' — this statement IS true. Intestinal (villus) mucosal cells are shed and replaced roughly every 3-5 days, among the fastest-renewing cell populations in the body.

## answer_c
Some di-and tri-peptides are hydrolyzed by intracellular peptidases to amino acids

## explanation_c
Incorrect as an answer to 'not true' — this statement IS true. After brush-border and luminal digestion, some remaining di- and tripeptides are absorbed intact and hydrolysed to free amino acids by cytoplasmic (intracellular) peptidases.

## answer_d
All of the above

## explanation_d
Incorrect, since only statement A (trehalase yields one glucose molecule) is false; B and C are true statements.
