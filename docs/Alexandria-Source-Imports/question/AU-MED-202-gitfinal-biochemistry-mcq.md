## title
Diagnosis: reduced GFR, big toe swelling and pain

## question
A 53-year old man admitted your clinic with sever pain during urination in addition to small amount of urine excreted. During last week, he noticed a swelling, redness, moderate pain in his big toe. On checking up results of kidney function tests, his GFR was 57 and Creatinine level in urine was lower that the normal. This patient may suffer from….

## subject
gi

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette
A 53-year-old man presents with painful urination and reduced urine output, plus a week of swelling, redness and moderate pain in his big toe. Kidney function tests show a reduced GFR (57) and lower-than-normal urinary creatinine.

## correct_answer
C

## answer_a
Bilharzial hepatic fibrosis

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Bilharzial hepatic fibrosis causes portal hypertension and liver-related signs, not an acutely painful, red, swollen big toe.

## answer_b
Liver cirrhosis

## explanation_b
Incorrect. Liver cirrhosis does not classically present with acute monoarticular big-toe pain and swelling; that presentation points to a joint, not hepatic, process.

## answer_c
Gout

## explanation_c
Correct. Acute gout presents with rapid joint pain, warmth, swelling, redness and marked tenderness, classically at the big toe (podagra); reduced renal function (lower GFR) reduces uric acid excretion, a recognised trigger for an acute attack.

## answer_d
Myocardial infarction

## explanation_d
Incorrect. Myocardial infarction presents with chest pain and cardiac signs, unrelated to an acutely inflamed big toe.

## title
Urine component increased after allopurinol

## question
After taking the previous drug what is the most likely component to be increased in urine?

## subject
gi

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette
Continuing the same patient after starting allopurinol for gout.

## correct_answer
D

## answer_a
Urate

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Allopurinol lowers, rather than raises, uric acid (urate) excretion, since blocking xanthine oxidase prevents its formation.

## answer_b
Inosine mono phosphate

## explanation_b
Incorrect. IMP is an early purine nucleotide intermediate, upstream of the catabolic pathway that xanthine oxidase inhibition affects; it is not the component that characteristically rises in urine after allopurinol.

## answer_c
Adenosine

## explanation_c
Incorrect. Adenosine catabolism proceeds through inosine and hypoxanthine before reaching the xanthine oxidase step; adenosine itself is not the component that rises after allopurinol.

## answer_d
Xanthine

## explanation_d
Correct. Allopurinol competitively inhibits xanthine oxidase, the enzyme that converts hypoxanthine to xanthine and xanthine to uric acid; blocking this final step diverts purine catabolism upstream, so xanthine (and hypoxanthine), both far more soluble than uric acid, accumulate and are excreted in greater amounts in urine.

Best treatment for megaloblastic anaemia with growth retardation

A

Taking uridine monophosphate containing drug

Correct. Hereditary orotic aciduria (UMP synthase deficiency) leaves a child unable to make its own pyrimidines, producing megaloblastic anaemia and growth/mental retardation; a uridine-containing drug bypasses the block, refills the pyrimidine pool and corrects the anaemia.

Weight loss

## answer_d
Taking carboamoyl phosphate containing drug

## explanation_d
Incorrect. Carbamoyl phosphate acts upstream of the UMP synthase block and would not relieve it; only a uridine-containing drug, acting downstream of the block, corrects the deficiency.

End products of pyrimidine catabolism

End products of pyrimidine catabolism are….

β- Aminoisobutyrate, H2O, HCOOH

## explanation_b
Incorrect. H2O and formic acid (HCOOH) are not recognised end products of pyrimidine catabolism; CO2 and beta-alanine are the correct companions to beta-aminoisobutyrate.

NH3, L- Serine, N- Acetyl glutamine

## explanation_d
Incorrect. L-serine and N-acetylglutamate are not pyrimidine catabolism end products; N-acetylglutamate instead activates carbamoyl phosphate synthetase I in the urea cycle.

## title
Converting UMP to dUMP

## question
To get dUMP from UMP , This occurs by .............

## subject
gi

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
A

## answer_a
Phosphorylation , Reduction and Dephosphorylation

## explanation_a
Correct. UMP is phosphorylated to UDP, UDP is reduced at the ribose 2'-carbon by ribonucleotide reductase to dUDP, and dUDP is then dephosphorylated to dUMP.

Phosphorylation , Oxidation and Dephosphorylation

## explanation_b
Incorrect. Ribonucleotide reductase performs a reduction, not an oxidation, of the ribose 2'-carbon.
