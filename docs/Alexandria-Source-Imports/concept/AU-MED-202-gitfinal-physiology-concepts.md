## label
Digestive juice pH is 7-7.5

## canonical_key
gi.digestivejuice.ph-7-to-7.5

## definition
Pooled small-intestinal digestive juice (succus entericus, together with the pancreatic and biliary secretions that mix with it) runs near-neutral to mildly alkaline, roughly pH 7-7.5, in contrast to the strongly acidic gastric juice (pH 1-3) it follows. This near-neutral to alkaline pH is what protects the duodenal mucosa and creates the optimum working range for pancreatic and brush-border digestive enzymes, which are inactivated by strong acid.

## explicit_objective
State that pooled intestinal digestive juice runs at roughly pH 7-7.5, distinguishing it from the strongly acidic gastric juice.

## label
The brush border is the main site of intestinal digestion

## canonical_key
intestine.digestion.brush-border-main-site

## definition
While luminal enzymes (pancreatic amylase, proteases and lipase) carry out the initial, bulk breakdown of a meal, the final digestive steps for carbohydrates and proteins — disaccharide and oligopeptide hydrolysis by brush-border enzymes such as maltase, sucrase, lactase and various peptidases — occur at the enterocyte's brush border membrane itself, immediately before the products are absorbed. This membrane (contact) digestion at the brush border, not free luminal digestion, is regarded as the main site of intestinal digestion.

## explicit_objective
Identify the brush border, not the intestinal lumen, cytoplasm or crypts of Lieberkuhn, as the main site of intestinal digestion.

VIP causes vasodilation, not vasoconstriction, of peripheral blood vessels

## canonical_key
gi.vip.vasodilation-not-vasoconstriction

## definition
Vasoactive intestinal peptide (VIP), released by enteric neurons, is a vasodilator of peripheral (including splanchnic and salivary gland) blood vessels, not a vasoconstrictor; its other actions include relaxing gastrointestinal smooth muscle, potentiating acetylcholine's effect on salivary secretion, and stimulating intestinal electrolyte (and so fluid) secretion. Vasoconstriction is therefore the one listed action that is NOT part of VIP's physiological role.

## explicit_objective
