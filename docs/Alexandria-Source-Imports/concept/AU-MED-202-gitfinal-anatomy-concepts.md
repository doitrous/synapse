Peritoneal covering of the large intestine's named parts

## canonical_key
colon.peritoneal-covering.transverse-sigmoid-complete-vs-ascending-descending-partial

## definition
The transverse colon and sigmoid colon are each suspended on their own mesocolon and so are completely covered by peritoneum (mobile, intraperitoneal). The ascending and descending colon are secondarily retroperitoneal, covered by peritoneum only on their anterior surface and sides, with a bare posterior area fixed to the posterior abdominal wall.

## explicit_objective
Distinguish the intraperitoneal (mesocolon-suspended) transverse and sigmoid colon from the secondarily retroperitoneal ascending and descending colon.
