## title
Sequence of the supraspinal pain control system

## question
Which of the following is the true sequence for the supraspinal control system of pain?

## subject
neuro

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette


## correct_answer
A

## answer_a
Periaqueductal grey >> nucleus raphe magnus >> pre and post synaptic fibers >> local cord neuron

## explanation_a
Correct. The periaqueductal grey initiates the descending analgesic system via its enkephalin-secreting neurons, which excite the nucleus raphe magnus; this relay's serotonergic and enkephalinergic projections then act on the pre- and post-synaptic elements of the pain pathway at the spinal dorsal horn, ultimately inhibiting the local cord (transmission) neuron. In short: identify PAG-to-raphe-magnus-to-pre/post-synaptic-fibres-to-local-cord-neuron as the correct descending sequence.

## answer_b
Nucleus raphe magnus >> Periaqueductal grey >> pre and post synaptic fibers>> local cord neuron

## explanation_b
Incorrect. This reverses the first two steps: the periaqueductal grey is the origin of the pathway and excites the nucleus raphe magnus, not the reverse.

## answer_c
Periaqueductal grey >> nucleus raphe magnus >> local cord neurons >> pre and post synaptic fibers

## explanation_c
Incorrect. This reverses the last two steps: the descending signal acts on the pre- and post-synaptic pain-fibre elements first, at the level of the local cord neuron's synapse, not on "local cord neurons" as a separate step before the synaptic elements.

## answer_d
None of the above

## explanation_d
Incorrect, since option A is the correct sequence.
