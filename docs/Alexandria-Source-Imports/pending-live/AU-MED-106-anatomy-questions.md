B

## answer_a
Sympathetic trunk

## explanation_a
Incorrect. The sympathetic trunk runs more laterally, on the rib necks/vertebral bodies, a different course from the vagus nerve pictured here.

## answer_b
Vagus nerve

## explanation_b
Correct. The right and left vagus nerves take different paths through the mediastinum before breaking up behind their lung roots into the pulmonary and oesophageal plexuses. This is the structure this image identifies on a posterior-mediastinum cross-section. Distinguishing it from the neighbouring sympathetic trunk, phrenic nerve and thoracic duct is the skill this item tests.

Phrenic nerve

## explanation_c
Incorrect. The phrenic nerve runs anterior to the lung root (on the venous side on the right, arterial side on the left), a different position from the vagus nerve pictured here.

## answer_d
Thoracic duct

## explanation_d
Incorrect. The thoracic duct lies against the vertebral bodies, crossing to the left at T5, a deeper midline structure distinct from the vagus nerve.
