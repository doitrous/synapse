## text
Five pairs of intrinsic muscles (plus the unpaired transverse arytenoid) move the vocal folds: the posterior cricoarytenoid abducts (the only abductor); the lateral cricoarytenoid and the arytenoid muscles adduct; the cricothyroid tenses and lengthens the folds (raising pitch) and is the one intrinsic muscle supplied by the external laryngeal nerve rather than the recurrent laryngeal nerve; the thyroarytenoid relaxes them.
