## explanation_a
The displayed broth-dilution series remains clear at 1.0 µg/ml but shows visible turbidity at 0.50 µg/ml. Because the MIC is the lowest tested concentration without visible growth, 0.50 µg/ml is not the MIC. The statement is therefore not true.
## answer_b
False
## explanation_b
The displayed broth-dilution series is clear through 1.0 µg/ml and becomes visibly turbid at 0.50 µg/ml. The MIC is the lowest tested concentration without visible growth, so the figure’s endpoint is 1.0 µg/ml and the literal statement that it is 0.50 µg/ml is false. The source highlights false as a teaching answer, not an official examination key.
