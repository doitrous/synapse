## title
Liver biopsy is used for diagnosis of:

## question
Liver biopsy is used for diagnosis of:

## subject
gi

## status
Draft

## owner
Helwan Year-3 authoring lane

## vignette

## correct_answer
A

## answer_a
Visceral larva migrans

## explanation_a
Liver biopsy is used for diagnosis of visceral larva migrans, the tissue-invasive larval infection caused by the dog/cat ascarid Toxocara, because histology can directly demonstrate the migrating larva together with its surrounding eosinophilic granulomatous reaction in liver tissue. This tissue-based approach is needed precisely because Toxocara larvae never mature into egg-laying adults in the human gut, so there is no stool egg stage to examine instead. Recognising visceral larva migrans as the option this hepatic tissue diagnosis specifically applies to is the fact tested here.

## answer_b
Fasciola hepatica

## explanation_b
Fasciola hepatica is instead diagnosed by stool or duodenal-aspirate egg examination and, in the early pre-patent phase, by serology, not routinely by liver biopsy.

## answer_c
Fasciola gigantica

## explanation_c
Fasciola gigantica is instead diagnosed by stool or duodenal-aspirate egg examination and, in the early pre-patent phase, by serology, not routinely by liver biopsy.

## answer_d
Clonorchis sinensis

## explanation_d
Clonorchis sinensis, the Chinese liver fluke, is diagnosed by finding its eggs in stool or duodenal aspirate, not routinely by liver biopsy.

## answer_e
All of the above

## explanation_e
Since Fasciola hepatica, Fasciola gigantica and Clonorchis sinensis are all diagnosed by egg examination rather than liver biopsy, "all of the above" is incorrect; liver biopsy applies specifically to the tissue-migrating Toxocara larva.

Recognising Ancylostoma duodenale as the exception to autoinfection capacity among these four intestinal parasites is the fact tested here.

Stool examination

## explanation_a
Stool examination is unreliable for enterobiasis, since Enterobius vermicularis eggs are deposited on perianal skin rather than shed into the intestinal lumen.

Peri-anal swab

## explanation_b
A reliable method for diagnosing enterobiasis is peri-anal swab, collected in the early morning before bathing or defecation when the gravid female has had overnight opportunity to deposit eggs on the perianal skin. This technique directly samples the actual egg-deposition site, making it far more sensitive for Enterobius vermicularis than a stool-based or tissue-based method aimed at a different anatomical location. Recognising peri-anal swab as the reliable diagnostic method for enterobiasis is the fact tested here.

Rectal snip is instead used for diagnosing schistosomiasis by finding ova within rectal mucosal tissue, not for enterobiasis.

## answer_d
Kato-Katz technique

## explanation_d
The Kato-Katz technique is a quantitative stool method used for soil-transmitted helminth and schistosome egg counts, not for diagnosing enterobiasis.

Ancylostoma caninum cannot establish an intestinal worm burden in man at all, producing only cutaneous larva migrans, so it cannot cause these mechanical obstruction complications.
