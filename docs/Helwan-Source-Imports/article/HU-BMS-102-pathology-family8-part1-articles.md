## summary
Acute inflammation is a rapid exudative response. Permeability and fluid escape produce haemoconcentration and slowing before leukocyte emigration; neutrophils dominate the first 48 hours and then engulf particles by phagocytosis.
## sections
### Definition
Acute inflammation is characterised by rapid onset, short duration and exudation rather than fibrosis.

### Mechanism
Leukocyte emigration follows vascular permeability, exudation and slowing in acute inflammation. Fluid loss from vessels concentrates blood cells, encourages slowing and margination, and prepares leukocytes to cross the wall.

### Key determinants
Neutrophils predominate during the first 48 hours of acute inflammation. Phagocytosis is cellular engulfment of particulate material.

### Clinical significance
Sequence questions should be solved by separating vascular events from later cellular events. Emigration precedes tissue chemotaxis and phagocytosis, while fibrosis signals repair or chronicity rather than the defining acute phase.

## summary
Mediator questions become manageable when each chemical is tied to one dominant action. C5a and LTB4 drive chemotaxis, kallikrein generates bradykinin and bradykinin mediates pain; morphology then distinguishes serofibrinous serosal inflammation and the clustered abscesses of a carbuncle.
## sections
### Definition
Angiogenesis is the formation of new blood vessels. A carbuncle is a cluster of deeply connected cutaneous abscesses.

### Mechanism
Complement C5a and leukotriene B4 promote leukocyte chemotaxis. Kallikrein forms bradykinin from a plasma precursor. Bradykinin mediates pain in acute inflammation.

### Key determinants
Serofibrinous inflammation affects serous membranes rather than mucous membranes. The anatomic surface distinguishes it from catarrhal and membranous mucosal patterns.

### Clinical significance
A multiloculated hot red neck lesion in diabetes points to a carbuncle. In mediator matching, identify the action first: chemotaxis selects C5a/LTB4, pain selects bradykinin, and the kinin pathway selects kallikrein.
