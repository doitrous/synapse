### Definition
Multiple myeloma is represented by lytic lesions, monoclonal protein and plasma-cell proliferation. Chondrosarcoma is represented by a destructive calcified chondroid mass with atypical chondrocytes in chondroid matrix. Osteoma is represented by a dense circumscribed mass composed of mature lamellar bone.

Q19 uses spinal destruction, popcorn calcification, a bluish-white cut surface and atypical chondrocytes to key chondrosarcoma. Q21 repeats the myeloma diagnostic cluster but asks directly for the diagnosis. Q28 describes a hard forehead mass and prints osteoma after histology shows mature lamellar bone.

These items extend the existing article without erasing Q4-Q7 or Q13-Q14. Each keeps its exact wording and option order. The Q28 phrase “with revealed” remains unchanged because source fidelity controls.

Do not substitute enchondroma for the malignant chondroid pattern. Do not merge Q21 with Q14’s prognostic objective. Do not rewrite Q28 grammar. All records require independent verification and faculty review.
