### Key determinants

- The source identifies the distal-femoral mushroom-shaped lesion and microscopic field as osteochondroma.
- The source identifies the multiple variably sized tan liver masses as metastatic adenocarcinoma.
- The source identifies the hair-filled ovarian mass with differentiated tissues from multiple germ layers as a mature teratoma.
- The source identifies infiltrating squamous nests breaching the basement membrane as invasive squamous cell carcinoma.
- The source identifies the forehead-nodule microscopic field as basal cell carcinoma.
- The source identifies marked pleomorphism, hyperchromasia and a bizarre tripolar mitosis as anaplastic morphology.
- The source identifies the giant-cell-rich knee-mass microscopic field as osteoclastoma.
