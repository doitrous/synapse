## sections
### Definition
Commensals may become opportunistic when they enter a new habitat. A carrier possesses and can transmit a microorganism without the clinical illness that would confine a patient to bed.

### Mechanism
Movement of gastrointestinal E. coli into the urinary tract creates the source’s opportunistic-infection scenario. Pili support E. coli attachment to urinary-tract epithelium. Chronic carriage describes prolonged or permanent carriage.

### Key determinants
Q01 applies habitat change to a specific organism and outcome; Q02 tests the general condition. Q03 classifies duration, while Q04 tests carrier characteristics. Q05 tests an adherence structure.

### Clinical significance
Normal-flora location, carrier state and bacterial adherence all influence whether organisms colonize, spread or cause disease.

### Common pitfalls
Do not treat normal flora as harmless at every site, confuse chronic carriage with transient states, or replace pili with motility or spore structures.

### Source-grounded claims
Normal gastrointestinal E. coli can cause urinary tract infection after entering the urinary tract.
Normal flora can cause disease after entering a new body site.
A chronic carrier may continue shedding an organism for years.
Bacterial carriers transmit microorganisms without clinical symptoms and are not confined to bed.
E. coli uses pili to attach to urinary-tract epithelium.

## sections
### Definition
Exotoxins are protein products, whereas endotoxin is lipopolysaccharide associated with the Gram-negative cell wall.

### Mechanism
Endotoxin triggers generalized mediator, complement and coagulation responses. The local teaching links these pathways to fever, hypotension and disseminated intravascular coagulation.

### Key determinants
Protein composition distinguishes exotoxin in Q07. Cell-wall association identifies endotoxin in Q08. Generalized rather than specific-organ action resolves Q09.

### Clinical significance
Composition and localization help explain why exotoxin and endotoxin differ in release, immune effects and patterns of injury.

### Common pitfalls
Do not import the held Q06 Protein A/IgA statement or Q10 high-antigenicity causality into these supported toxin properties.

### Source-grounded claims
Exotoxins are proteins in nature.
Endotoxin is an integral bacterial cell-wall component.
Endotoxin causes generalized effects rather than a specific-organ effect.
