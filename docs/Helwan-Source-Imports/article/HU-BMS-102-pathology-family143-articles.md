### Definition
Five cases connect regeneration, permanent cells, primary closure, ulceration and keloid formation.

### Mechanism
Each teaching relation is preserved exactly at the scope of its printed case and explicit reveal.

### Key determinants
- Persistence of epithelial skin appendages permits surface epidermal regeneration after a partial-thickness burn.
- The source defines loss of an epithelial surface with inflammation as ulceration.
- Skeletal muscle is source-keyed as a permanent cell population that remains in G0 after injury.
- The source states that the net effect of sutures is to aid healing by closing the wound and reducing the space and migration distance to be bridged.
- The source identifies the collagen-rich post-wound mass as keloid formation caused by exaggerated collagenization.

### Clinical significance
The cases connect morphology and mechanism without extending the carrier into patient-care guidance.

### Common misconceptions
Do not treat a CBL reveal as an administered examination or official university key.

### Definition
Nine distinct-carrier replays connect thrombosis, embolism, infarction and DIC.

### Mechanism
Each teaching relation is preserved exactly at the scope of its printed case and explicit reveal.

### Key determinants
- Immobilization during hospitalization is the source-supplied strongest contributor through venous stasis.
- The source explains the postprandial pain as abdominal angina from mesenteric atherosclerotic occlusion.
- The source keys cold water fish oil and states that fish oils diminish arachidonic-acid metabolites and reduce platelet aggregation.
- The retained cell outlines in an ischemic solid-organ infarct are source-explained as coagulative necrosis.
- The source links hip-prosthesis surgery and immobilization to venous thrombosis with subsequent pulmonary embolization.
- The source identifies pancreatic adenocarcinoma and explains its Trousseau hypercoagulable effect through thromboplastic substances.
- The source keys organization and states that an organizing thrombus can be removed over time with restoration of the vascular lumen.
- The source links gram-negative endotoxaemia to endothelial injury and widespread coagulation activation causing DIC.
- The source keys adenocarcinoma and explains malignancy-associated Trousseau hypercoagulability.

The cases connect morphology and mechanism without extending the carrier into patient-care guidance.
