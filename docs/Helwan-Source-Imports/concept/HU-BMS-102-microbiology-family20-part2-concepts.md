## definition
Bacteria may be phenotypically resistant when they are walled off inside an abscess cavity that an antimicrobial cannot penetrate effectively. This is a non-genetic access problem rather than plasmid mutation, enzyme degradation or target modification.
## explicit_objective
Identify poor antimicrobial penetration as the source mechanism of resistance in a walled-off abscess.
## pitfalls
Treating the abscess setting as proof of plasmid mutation, drug-inactivating enzyme production or target modification.
