Family223 Q66 assumes total body water is 60% of 70 kg, giving 42 L, then multiplies by a target 5 mg/L to obtain 210 mg. This is a source-bounded educational calculation, not a clinical dose recommendation.
## explicit_objective
Reproduce the source exercise by estimating total body water and multiplying it by the stated target concentration.
## pitfalls
Using body weight directly as litres, missing the 60% assumption, or converting this educational result into a patient-specific prescription.
