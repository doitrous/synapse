## explicit_objective

## definition
The Gleason grading system assesses architectural differentiation in prostate adenocarcinoma. A combined score of 9 represents a high-grade, poorly differentiated tumour with aggressive biological behaviour.
## explicit_objective
Interpret a Gleason score of 9 as highly aggressive prostate cancer.
## pitfalls
Calling a high Gleason score well differentiated, organ-confined or unlikely to metastasise.
