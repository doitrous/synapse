## definition
Brown induration is the chronic pulmonary-congestion pattern produced by persistent venous outflow impairment, classically in left-sided heart failure or mitral stenosis. Repeated capillary leakage, haemosiderin-laden macrophages and fibrosis make the lung brown and firm.
## explicit_objective
Recognise left-sided heart failure and mitral stenosis as source-keyed settings for brown induration of the lung.
## pitfalls
Assigning systemic hepatic congestion to left-sided failure, or treating the tier-3 printed letter as an official examination key.

## definition
Oedema is abnormal accumulation of fluid in interstitial tissue spaces or body cavities. It differs from hyperaemia, which is increased blood flow; haemorrhage, which is escape of blood from vessels or chambers; and embolism, which is circulation of detached intravascular material.

Define oedema by the location and abnormal accumulation of fluid.
