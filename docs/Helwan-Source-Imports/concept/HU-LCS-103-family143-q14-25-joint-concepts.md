## explicit_objective
Recognise the exact source cues linking recurrent great-toe attacks, monosodium urate and podagra.

## pitfalls
Do not substitute calcium pyrophosphate, which the same source assigns to pseudogout. Do not move podagra away from gout of the great toe.
