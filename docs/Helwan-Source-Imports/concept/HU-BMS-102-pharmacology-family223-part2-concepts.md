Adrenaline is the international nonproprietary name commonly used in many countries, while epinephrine is the United States adopted name for the same drug molecule.

## label
Among the printed choices, amphetamine is least absorbed in the acidic stomach because its high-pKa weak base is predominantly ionised
## id
CON-FND-34E605BD473761
## canonical_key
teaching.pharma.absorption.stomach-weak-base-high-pka
## definition
In the acidic stomach, a weak base with a high pKa is predominantly protonated and ionised. The printed option set therefore keys amphetamine (pKa 9.9) as the least absorbed there, because the ionised fraction crosses lipid membranes poorly.
## explicit_objective
Use acid–base ionisation and the printed pKa values to identify the least stomach-absorbed option.
## pitfalls
Reading the highest pKa as the highest absorption regardless of pH. For a weak base in an acidic compartment, greater protonation means less non-ionised drug available for passive transfer.
