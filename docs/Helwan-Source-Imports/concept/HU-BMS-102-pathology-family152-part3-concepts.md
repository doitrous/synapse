## definition
Chronic pulmonary venous congestion causes alveolar capillary rupture and leakage of erythrocytes into alveoli. Alveolar macrophages ingest the erythrocytes, degrade haemoglobin and accumulate haemosiderin; these siderophages are called heart-failure cells.
## explicit_objective

## pitfalls
Choosing neutrophils, hyaline membranes or necrotising vasculitis when the vignette specifically indicates chronic mitral-stenosis congestion.
