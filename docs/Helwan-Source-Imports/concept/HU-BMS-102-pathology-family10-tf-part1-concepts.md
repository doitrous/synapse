## definition
Inflammation is the local vascular, lymphatic and cellular response of living tissue to an injurious agent. It is a protective reaction that removes or contains the cause of injury and initiates clearance and repair.
## explicit_objective
Define inflammation as a local response of living tissue to an injurious agent.
## pitfalls
Calling inflammation a systemic reaction only, or applying the term to dead tissue that cannot mount a vascular and cellular response.
