## definition
In a valid broth-dilution series, the minimum inhibitory concentration is the lowest tested antimicrobial concentration at which visible microbial growth is inhibited. The endpoint is read at the lowest clear tube, immediately before visible turbidity appears as concentration decreases.
## explicit_objective
Read a serial broth-dilution figure and identify the lowest clear concentration as the MIC endpoint.
## pitfalls
Choosing the first turbid tube, ignoring concentration direction or controls, or interpreting an image without confirming that the concentration labels and growth pattern are visible.
