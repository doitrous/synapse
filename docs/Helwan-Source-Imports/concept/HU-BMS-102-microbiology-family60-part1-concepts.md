## definition
Nutrient agar is a general-purpose solid medium formed by adding agar as a solidifying agent to a nutrient-broth base. Agar provides the solid matrix and is not substituted by gelatin or sugar in the source distinction.
## explicit_objective
Identify nutrient broth plus agar as the defining composition of nutrient agar.
## pitfalls
Selecting water plus agar, substituting gelatin, or treating sugar as the defining solidifying component.
