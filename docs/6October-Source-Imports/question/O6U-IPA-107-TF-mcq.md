## title
Portal hypertension and pulmonary bilharziasis

## question
Portal hypertension complicating hepatic bilharziasis can predispose to which of the following?

## subject
fnd

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
A

## answer_a
Pulmonary bilharziasis, via portosystemic shunting

## explanation_a
Correct. Hepatic bilharziasis raises portal pressure, and that portal hypertension opens portosystemic venous shunts. Schistosome ova can then bypass the liver's normal filtering and reach the lungs through those shunts, producing pulmonary bilharziasis. This is why pulmonary involvement is seen specifically in patients with the hepatosplenic (portal-hypertensive) form of the disease, not simply in anyone infected with schistosomes.

## answer_b
Primary lung carcinoma

## explanation_b
Incorrect. Portal hypertension does not cause primary lung carcinoma; the recognised pulmonary complication of hepatic bilharziasis is pulmonary bilharziasis itself, via shunted ova.

## answer_c
Pulmonary tuberculosis specifically

## explanation_c
Incorrect. Portal hypertension has no specific mechanistic link to pulmonary tuberculosis.

## answer_d
No pulmonary complications at all

## explanation_d
Incorrect. Pulmonary bilharziasis via portosystemic shunting is a recognised complication of portal hypertension in hepatic bilharziasis.

Schistosomiasis liver change

## question
Which best describes the hepatic change caused by schistosomiasis?

## subject
fnd

A

## answer_a
Periportal ("pipe-stem") fibrosis, distinct from true cirrhosis

## explanation_a
Correct. Hepatic schistosomiasis produces periportal (pipe-stem) fibrosis around portal tracts, a presinusoidal pattern of fibrosis. True cirrhosis is defined by diffuse fibrosis together with nodular regeneration, which the schistosomal pattern lacks — so it is taught specifically as a distinct entity from cirrhosis rather than a form of it. The distinction matters because the two conditions carry different natural histories and monitoring needs.

Micronodular cirrhosis

Incorrect. Schistosomal liver fibrosis does not show the nodular regeneration that defines micronodular cirrhosis.

Macronodular cirrhosis

## explanation_c
Incorrect. Schistosomal liver fibrosis does not show the nodular regeneration that defines macronodular cirrhosis either.

## answer_d
Fatty change without any fibrosis

## explanation_d
Incorrect. Schistosomiasis causes fibrosis, specifically periportal fibrosis, not isolated fatty change without fibrosis.

Incorrect. Hypersplenism is a well-recognised complication of portal-hypertensive liver diseases, including hepatic bilharzial fibrosis.
