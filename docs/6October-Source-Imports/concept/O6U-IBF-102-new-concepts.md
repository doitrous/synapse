## label
Severe muscular exercise produces metabolic acidosis

## canonical_key
exercise.acidbase.metabolic-acidosis

## definition
Severe or prolonged muscular exercise outpaces oxidative metabolism's oxygen supply, so
muscle shifts to anaerobic glycolysis and produces lactic acid faster than it can be
buffered or cleared. The resulting drop in blood pH from a fixed (non-volatile) acid load is
a metabolic, not respiratory, acidosis.

## explicit_objective
Classify exercise-induced acidosis as metabolic (from lactic acid accumulation), not respiratory.

## label
Sympathomimetics relieve bronchial asthma by dilating the airway

## canonical_key
sympathomimetic.indication.bronchial-asthma

## definition
Bronchial asthma narrows the airway through bronchial smooth-muscle contraction.
Sympathomimetics (beta-2 adrenergic agonists) relax bronchial smooth muscle, producing
bronchodilation, which is why they -- not sympatholytics, parasympathomimetics or an
alpha-blocker such as phentolamine -- are the drug class used to treat an asthma attack.

## explicit_objective
