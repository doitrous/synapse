## label
A defective virus lacks a complete genome and needs a helper virus to complete productive multiplication

## canonical_key
virology.defective-virus.needs-helper-virus

## definition
A defective virus is a virus particle that does not contain sufficient/complete nucleic acid to independently direct a full replication cycle. Because it cannot code for everything it needs (such as a functional coat protein) on its own, it depends on co-infection with a fully competent "helper" virus to supply the missing function before it can complete productive multiplication and produce new infectious particles.

## explicit_objective
State that a defective virus needs co-infection with a helper virus to complete productive multiplication, because it lacks sufficient nucleic acid to replicate independently.
