Mention the types of autonomic ganglia.
Expects: Paravertebral sympathetic chain: on both sides of the vertebral column, with a ganglion for each segment of the spinal cord
Expects: Only three ganglia serve the cervical region — superior, middle and inferior cervical — and the chain relays sympathetic fibres only
Expects: Collateral ganglia: midway between the spinal cord and the viscera, at the origin of the big vessels arising from the abdominal aorta, and named after those vessels
Expects: Collateral ganglia relaying sympathetic fibres: coeliac, superior mesenteric and inferior mesenteric
Expects: Collateral ganglia relaying parasympathetic fibres: ciliary, sphenopalatine, submaxillary and otic
Expects: Terminal ganglia: near or in the visceral organ itself, relaying parasympathetic fibres, whose postganglionic fibres are very short — 1 mm to several centimetres
Expects: The adrenal medulla: a modified sympathetic ganglion whose postganglionic cells have lost their axons and secrete catecholamines directly into the blood stream
Expects: It is supplied by preganglionic cholinergic sympathetic fibres, and its stimulation releases adrenaline (80%) and noradrenaline (20%)
