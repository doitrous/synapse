## written_parts
### (a) 5 marks
Point out the boundaries and contents of the adductor canal.
Expects: An intermuscular tunnel on the medial aspect of the middle third of the thigh; begins at the apex of the femoral triangle and ends at the adductor hiatus
Expects: Boundaries — the posterior wall (floor) is adductor longus and adductor magnus
Expects: Boundaries — the antero-lateral wall is vastus medialis
Expects: Boundaries — the antero-medial wall is a fibrous roof between vastus medialis and adductor magnus, covered by sartorius
Expects: Contents — the femoral artery, entering through the femoral triangle apex and leaving via the adductor hiatus to enter the popliteal fossa, giving a descending genicular branch in the canal
Expects: Contents — the femoral vein, entering through the adductor hiatus and leaving at the femoral triangle apex
Expects: Contents — the saphenous nerve, crossing the femoral artery from lateral to medial to leave the canal by piercing the roof
Expects: Contents — the nerve to vastus medialis
Concept: CON-MSK-59755B64721E3D

Expects: The circumflex fibular artery, curving around the neck of the fibula and sharing in the anastomosis around the knee

Expects: Muscular branches to the calf muscles
Expects: A nutrient artery to the tibia
Expects: Medial malleolar and medial calcaneal branches, sharing in the anastomosis around the medial malleolus
Expects: Terminal branches — the medial and lateral plantar arteries
