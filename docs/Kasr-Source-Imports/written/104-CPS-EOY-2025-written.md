Describe the ionic bases of phase 1 and phase 2 in cardiac myocyte action potential (4 marks)
Expects: Phase 1: rapid small repolarization from inactivation of fast Na+ channels and transient outward K+ current (Ito)
Expects: Phase 2: plateau held near zero mV for about 200 msec
Expects: Outward positive current in phase 2 is K+ efflux through delayed rectifier channels

Expects: Semilunar valves are open in both phases
Expects: Ventricular pressure increases in rapid ejection, to about 120 mmHg in the left ventricle and 25 mmHg in the right, and decreases in reduced ejection
Expects: Ventricular volume falls rapidly in rapid ejection and slowly in reduced ejection, reaching an end-systolic volume of about 60 ml
Expects: Ejection continues in the reduced phase only because the momentum of the ejected blood keeps it moving forwards
Expects: Arterial pressure rises to its peak during rapid ejection and falls during reduced ejection, ending in the dicrotic notch as the valves close
Concept: CON-CVS-A70930DB23A5B4

Expects: Expansion tendency of the chest wall: elasticity of its muscles, tendons and ligaments
Expects: Relaxation volume of the lung is about 1 litre, of the thorax about 5 litres
Expects: At the end of expiration the combined volume is about 2.3 litres, so the lung is stretched and recoils and the thorax is compressed and expands
Expects: The two opposing tendencies are what make intrapleural pressure negative
