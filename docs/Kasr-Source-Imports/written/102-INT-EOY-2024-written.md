Regarding plasma proteins, explain the site of their formation {2 Marks} and mention six of their functions {6 Marks}.
Expects: Site: the liver is the main site of synthesis of plasma proteins — albumin, fibrinogen, the coagulation factors and 50% of the globulins are formed there
Expects: Site: the gamma globulins are formed by plasma cells in the lymphoid tissue
Expects: Osmotic function: the capillary membrane is impermeable to plasma proteins, so the 25 to 28 mmHg they contribute is the effective colloidal osmotic pressure that draws tissue fluid into the blood; albumin is the main protein responsible because it is present in the greatest concentration

Expects: Blood clotting: fibrinogen and all the clotting factors present in plasma are responsible for blood coagulation
Expects: Blood viscosity: blood is three times and plasma 1.5 times as viscous as water; fibrinogen contributes more than the other proteins because of its elongated shape, and viscosity maintains normal arterial blood pressure
Expects: Capillary function: plasma proteins close the pores in the capillary wall and so maintain normal capillary permeability; deficiency of plasma proteins increases it
Expects: Transport and conservation: albumin and the alpha and beta globulins carry hormones, vitamins, lipids and minerals, and binding prevents their rapid loss in urine and provides a reservoir
Expects: Source of amino acids: plasma proteins allow rapid replacement of tissue protein when tissue protein is depleted
