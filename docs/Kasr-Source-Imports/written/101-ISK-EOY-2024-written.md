Explain the different parts of the deep fascia and the function of each.
Expects: Deep fascia is a non-elastic membrane formed of compact regular collagen fibres
Expects: It forms broad sheets around groups of muscles
Expects: Those sheets give muscle attachment, fix underlying structures in position and help venous return
Expects: It forms intermuscular septa and interosseous membranes

Expects: The retinacula keep the tendons in position
Expects: It forms the palmar aponeurosis in the palm and the plantar aponeurosis in the sole
Expects: These are very thick strong layers protecting the underlying vessels, nerves and tendons
Expects: It forms fibrous sheaths around big vessels, e.g. the carotid sheath around the common carotid artery, internal jugular vein and vagus
