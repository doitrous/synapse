# Item

## term
Sternal angle

## ar
الزاوية القصية (زاوية لويس)

## category
Directional & anatomy

## def
The ridge felt where the manubrium meets the body of the breastbone, marking the level of the second rib, the tracheal bifurcation, and the start of the aortic arch.

## definition_ar
الحافة البارزة عند التقاء المقبض بجسم عظمة القص، وهي تمثل مستوى الضلع الثاني وتفرع القصبة الهوائية وبداية قوس الأبهر.

## example
A clinician counts ribs by first locating the sternal angle and palpating laterally to the second costal cartilage.

## id
sternal-angle

## example
Surgeons often harvest the internal thoracic artery as a graft for coronary bypass.
