Cross-check notes (key -> the option it selects, verified against the stem):
  Q1 A nothing per ear until spontaneous healing (traumatic perforation, keep the ear dry);
  Q2 D all of the above (bleeding per ear + usually central pars-tensa + irregular edges);
  Q3 D incision and evacuation (auricular haematoma drained to prevent cauliflower ear);
  Q4 A irregular edges of the perforation (traumatic hallmark);
  Q5 C marginal (EXCEPT — traumatic perforations are central, not marginal);
  Q6 B CSF otorrhoea (watery discharge increased by straining);
  Q7 D glomus jugulare tumour (pulsatile tinnitus);
  Q8 A unilateral tinnitus with progressive SNHL (commonest acoustic-neuroma symptom);
  Q9 E all of the above (CT/MRI petrous, blood sugar & ESR, isotope bone scan, culture);
  Q10 B otomycosis (canal full of white debris with blackish spots = Aspergillus);
  Q11 E all of the above (pseudomonas, diabetic/low-immunity, skull-base osteomyelitis, quinolones);
  Q12 B treated by local antifungal ear drops (otomycosis);
  Q13 C fungal otitis externa (otomycosis = another name);
  Q14 B malignant otitis externa (uncontrolled diabetes in the elderly);
  Q15 D itching (main symptom of otomycosis);
  Q16 E A & C (control of diabetes + antibiotics in large doses; not radiotherapy);
  Q18 D otomycosis (EXCEPT — bacterial OE causes furunculosis/diffuse/malignant OE; otomycosis is fungal).
