## summary
This cluster covers the renal-physiology and acid-base facts tested in the 2021 206 DIG End-of-Module paper. Glomerular filtration is driven by a net pressure of about 10 mmHg, and the filtration fraction (GFR/renal plasma flow) is about 0.2; constricting the afferent arteriole lowers GFR. Tubuloglomerular feedback uses adenosine released by the macula densa to constrict the afferent arteriole. The water-impermeable thick ascending limb dilutes tubular fluid and builds the medullary gradient, which ADH exploits by inserting aquaporin-2 into collecting-duct principal cells to concentrate the urine. Acid-base handling proceeds by secondary active Na+/H+ counter-transport in the proximal tubule and by ammonia buffering of secreted H+ distally; the lungs give rapid pH control through PCO2, while hyperaldosteronism (Conn’s) causes a metabolic alkalosis. Thermoregulatory sweating, an active cholinergic process, begins near an ambient temperature of 32-34 degrees C.
