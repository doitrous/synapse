Digestion and absorption of carbohydrates and lipids

## summary
Carbohydrate digestion begins in the mouth with salivary amylase and is completed in the small intestine; monosaccharides are then absorbed by specific transporters, while insulin-dependent GLUT4 governs glucose entry into muscle and fat, dietary pentoses go unused for lack of a specific kinase, and indigestible cellulose acts as bulk-forming fibre. Lipid digestion depends on bile-salt emulsification of fat into fine droplets and micelles, whose amphipathic molecules orient hydrophilic heads toward water and hydrophobic tails inward; absorbed fat is re-esterified (a fatty acyl-CoA is added to diacylglycerol by DGAT) and exported as chylomicrons.

### Mechanism
Salivary then pancreatic amylase and brush-border disaccharidases reduce starch to monosaccharides; glucose/galactose enter by SGLT1 and fructose by GLUT5, while muscle and adipose glucose uptake is by insulin-recruited GLUT4. Dietary pentoses are absorbed but not phosphorylated, and cellulose passes undigested as fibre. For fat, bile salts emulsify triacylglycerol so pancreatic lipase (activated by colipase) can act; digestion products enter mixed micelles, are absorbed, re-esterified to triacylglycerol (DGAT adding fatty acyl-CoA to diacylglycerol), and packaged into chylomicrons for lymphatic transport.
### Key determinants
Each item turns on one discriminator: GLUT4 is insulin dependent (unlike gut/kidney SGLT1); pentoses fail for want of a kinase, not for lack of absorption; cellulose is fibre, not fuel; carbohydrate digestion starts in the mouth; dietary fat travels as chylomicrons (not luminal micelles or albumin-bound fatty acids); the final triacylglycerol bond uses fatty acyl-CoA; bile salts emulsify rather than catalyse; and micelles orient polar heads outward.
### Clinical significance
These facts underlie lactose and other disaccharidase intolerances, the fat malabsorption and steatorrhoea of bile-salt or pancreatic-lipase deficiency, the role of dietary fibre in bowel health, and the postprandial chylomicronaemia seen when chylomicron clearance fails.
