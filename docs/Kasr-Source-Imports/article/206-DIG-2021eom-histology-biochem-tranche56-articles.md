Digestive-tract histology from the 2021 EOM paper: small intestine, anal canal, stomach and salivary glands
## subject
gi
## status

## owner

## topic

## summary
This cluster covers the gastrointestinal-histology facts tested in the 2021 206 DIG End-of-Module paper. The plicae circulares are folds with a submucosal core (unlike the mucosa-only villi), and the crypts of Lieberkuhn are simple tubular glands lying between the villus bases. At the pectinate line of the anal canal the muscularis mucosae breaks up and disappears. The acid-secreting parietal (oxyntic) cell is recognised by its intracellular canaliculus and tubulovesicular system, and the submandibular gland is a predominantly serous mixed gland draining through Wharton’s duct.

Urinary-system histology from the 2021 EOM paper: mesangial cells, renal tubules and the urethra
## subject
renal
## status
Draft
## owner
Claude
## topic
Histology
## summary
This cluster covers the urinary-system histology facts tested in the 2021 206 DIG End-of-Module paper. Mesangial cells are contractile, phagocytic supporting cells of the glomerulus, present both intraglomerularly and as lacis cells at the vascular pole. The collecting duct’s intercalated cells occur as alpha (acid-secreting) and beta (bicarbonate-secreting) types among the principal cells, and the distal convoluted tubule is recognised by extensive basolateral interdigitations with indistinct cell boundaries and no brush border. The prostatic urethra is lined by transitional epithelium (urothelium).

This cluster covers the digestion-and-absorption biochemistry tested in the 2021 206 DIG End-of-Module paper, a section that is genuinely fresh because the 2025 paper’s biochemistry was entirely lipid/liver metabolism. Lactose intolerance is due to intestinal lactase deficiency; chylomicrons are composed mainly of triacylglycerol; the end product of protein digestion is free amino acids; cholecystokinin is a hormonal stimulus of the pancreas rather than a constituent of pancreatic juice; and pancreatic trypsin hyposecretion is the enzyme defect that most directly impairs protein digestion in pancreatic insufficiency.
