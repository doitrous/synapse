## title
Myasthenia gravis is caused by anti-acetylcholine-receptor antibodies
## question
Myasthenia gravis is a disease characterized by:
## subject
mul
## status
Draft
## owner
Claude
## vignette

## correct_answer
A
## answer_a
Autoantibodies against the acetylcholine receptors
## explanation_a
Correct. Myasthenia gravis is an autoimmune disease caused by autoantibodies directed against the nicotinic acetylcholine receptors of the neuromuscular junction.
## answer_b
Constant ptosis and intermittent diplopia
## explanation_b
Incorrect. Myasthenic ptosis is fatigable and variable, not constant; both ptosis and diplopia fluctuate.
## answer_c
Improved ptosis with hot packs applied to the lids
## explanation_c
Incorrect. Cooling (the ice-pack test) improves myasthenic ptosis; heat worsens it.
## answer_d
Amsilon test is diagnostic
## explanation_d
Incorrect. There is no "Amsilon" test; the edrophonium (Tensilon) test is the relevant pharmacological test.
