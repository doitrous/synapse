## title
Vessels commonly involved by polyarteritis nodosa

## question
Polyarteritis nodosa (PAN) commonly involves:

## subject
cvs

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
B

## answer_a
Large arteries

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Polyarteritis nodosa spares the large elastic arteries such as the aorta; large-vessel involvement points instead to a large-vessel arteritis (e.g. Takayasu or giant cell arteritis).

## answer_b
Small and medium-sized arteries

## explanation_b
Correct. Polyarteritis nodosa is a necrotising vasculitis that characteristically involves the SMALL and MEDIUM-SIZED muscular arteries. The segmental transmural inflammation of these muscular arteries weakens the wall and produces the nodular microaneurysms that give the disease its name. It does not primarily affect large elastic arteries, arterioles or venules, which separates it from large-vessel arteritides and from small-vessel (microscopic) vasculitides.

## answer_c
Arterioles

## explanation_c
Incorrect. Arteriolar involvement is the province of small-vessel vasculitides such as microscopic polyangiitis or hypersensitivity vasculitis, not classic polyarteritis nodosa.

## answer_d
Venules

## explanation_d
Incorrect. Venules are affected by small-vessel leukocytoclastic (hypersensitivity) vasculitis, not by polyarteritis nodosa, which targets muscular arteries.

Cause of death in acute tearing chest pain radiating to back

## question
A 55 year old female hypertensive complains of severe chest pain of sudden onset radiating to back between scapula, deteriorating rapidly and dies. What is the most probable cause of death?

## subject
cvs

## status

## owner

## vignette

## correct_answer
B

## answer_a
Aortic aneurysm pressure on backbone

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Pressure erosion of the vertebrae by a chronic aneurysm is a slow, chronic process; it does not produce sudden tearing chest pain and rapid death.

## answer_b
Aortic dissecting aneurysm

## explanation_b
Correct. Sudden severe chest pain radiating to the back between the scapulae in a HYPERTENSIVE patient, with rapid deterioration and death, is the classic presentation of an AORTIC DISSECTING ANEURYSM (aortic dissection). Hypertension drives a tear in the aortic media; the propagating false lumen can rupture into the pericardium (tamponade) or mediastinum, causing rapid death. The tearing, interscapular, back-radiating pain in a hypertensive patient is the hallmark that points to dissection rather than the other options.

Primary cardiomyopathy

## explanation_c
Incorrect. Primary cardiomyopathy causes chronic heart failure or arrhythmic death, not sudden tearing interscapular pain.

## answer_d
Acute endocarditis

## explanation_d
Incorrect. Acute endocarditis presents with fever, murmur and embolic phenomena, not sudden tearing back pain from a hypertensive aorta.

## title
Not a true aneurysm (pulsating haematoma)

## question
The followings are true aneurysms except:

## subject
cvs

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
A

## answer_a
Pulsating hematoma

## explanation_a
Correct. This is the exception: a PULSATING HAEMATOMA is a FALSE aneurysm (pseudoaneurysm), not a true aneurysm. In a false aneurysm the wall is breached and the escaping blood is contained only by adventitia or perivascular tissue, with a pulsatile communication to the lumen. A true aneurysm, by contrast, is bounded by an attenuated but intact vessel wall, so a pulsating haematoma does not belong with the true aneurysms.

## answer_b
Mycotic aneurysm

## explanation_b
Incorrect (this IS a true aneurysm). A mycotic aneurysm is a true aneurysm in which the intact but infected wall is weakened and dilates.

## answer_c
Syphilitic aneurysm

## explanation_c
Incorrect (this IS a true aneurysm). A syphilitic aneurysm of the ascending aorta is a true aneurysm bounded by the (damaged) wall.

## answer_d
Dissecting aneurysm

## explanation_d
Incorrect (this IS classed with the aneurysms here). A dissecting aneurysm has blood within the layers of an intact wall, so it is grouped with the true aneurysms against the false pulsating haematoma.
