## title
General law regulating the Egyptian medical profession

## question
One of the following is a General Law that regulates the Egyptian medical profession:

## subject
mul

## status

## owner

## vignette

## correct_answer
A

## answer_a
The Penal Code

## explanation_a
Correct. The Penal Code is a GENERAL law — it applies to everyone and to all conduct, including doctors, and so it is one of the general laws that regulate the medical profession. General laws are distinguished from special (specific) laws that are written to govern a particular activity. Because the Penal Code governs criminal liability for all citizens, it counts as a general law bearing on medical practice.

## answer_b
Profession Ethics Regulations 238/2003

## explanation_b
Incorrect. The Profession Ethics Regulations 238/2003 are a special regulation issued specifically for the medical profession, not a general law.

## answer_c
Law No. 415/1954 for practice of the medical profession

## explanation_c
Incorrect. Law No. 415/1954 for the practice of the medical profession is the classic SPECIAL law governing medical practice specifically, not a general law.

## answer_d
None of the above

## explanation_d
Incorrect. Option A (the Penal Code) is a valid general law, so 'none of the above' is wrong.
