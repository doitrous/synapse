## title
Congenital heart disease with a right-to-left shunt

## question
The following congenital heart disease is with a right-to-left shunt:

## subject
cvs

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
B

## answer_a
Atrial septal defect.

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Atrial septal defect is an acyanotic LEFT-to-right shunt; reversal to right-to-left (Eisenmenger) is a late complication, not its usual state.

## answer_b
Fallot's tetralogy.

## explanation_b
Correct. FALLOT'S TETRALOGY is the classic congenital heart disease with a RIGHT-to-LEFT shunt, so it is CYANOTIC from the outset. The pulmonary stenosis raises right ventricular pressure and, together with the ventricular septal defect and the overriding aorta, drives deoxygenated blood from the right side directly into the systemic circulation. This right-to-left shunt is what produces central cyanosis, clubbing and secondary polycythaemia.

## answer_c
Ventricular septal defect (VSD).

## explanation_c
Incorrect. An uncomplicated VSD is an acyanotic LEFT-to-right shunt; it reverses to right-to-left only after Eisenmenger physiology develops.

## answer_d
Pulmonary artery stenosis.

## explanation_d
Incorrect. Pulmonary artery stenosis is an obstructive lesion, not in itself a shunt; it contributes to the right-to-left shunt only as one component of Fallot's tetralogy.

The most common type of cardiomyopathy is:

## subject

## answer_a
Dilated cardiomyopathy.

## explanation_a
Correct. DILATED cardiomyopathy is the COMMONEST type of cardiomyopathy — the same fact as the earlier 'congestive cardiomyopathy' item, since dilated and congestive are synonyms. It dilates all four chambers and impairs systolic function. Hypertrophic and restrictive forms are less common.

## answer_b
Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy.

## explanation_b
Incorrect. Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy is less common than the dilated form.

## answer_c
Restrictive cardiomyopathy.

## explanation_c
Incorrect. Restrictive cardiomyopathy is the least common of the three primary types.

## answer_d
None of the above.

## explanation_d
Incorrect. Dilated cardiomyopathy is a valid commonest answer, so 'none of the above' is wrong.
