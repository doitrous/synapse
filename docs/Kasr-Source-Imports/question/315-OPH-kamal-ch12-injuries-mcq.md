## title
Most important treatment of traumatic hyphema
## question
In the presence of hyphema due to blunt trauma, the MOST important treatment is:
## subject
mul
## status
Draft
## owner
Claude
## vignette

## correct_answer
B
## answer_a
Antibiotics.
## explanation_a
Incorrect. Antibiotics do not address the sight-threatening rise in intraocular pressure that complicates hyphema.
## answer_b
Anti-glaucoma medications.
## explanation_b
Correct. The most important treatment is anti-glaucoma medication to control the secondary rise in intraocular pressure from blood in the anterior chamber.
## answer_c
Anti-viral drugs.
## explanation_c
Incorrect. There is no viral element in a traumatic hyphema.
## answer_d
Lubricant eye drops.
## explanation_d
Incorrect. Lubricants soothe the surface but do nothing for the raised pressure.

## title
First aid in a chemical eye injury
## question
In case of chemical injury to the eye, the first aid is to:
## subject
mul
## status
Draft
## owner
Claude
## vignette

## correct_answer
B
## answer_a
Refer to the nearest ophthalmology clinic.
## explanation_a
Incorrect. Referral must not delay the immediate irrigation that limits the burn.
## answer_b
Wash copiously with tap water and refer to ophthalmologist.
## explanation_b
Correct. Immediate copious irrigation with tap water (or saline) dilutes and removes the chemical and is the first aid before referral.
## answer_c
Give topical and systemic antibiotics and refer to ophthalmologists.
## explanation_c
Incorrect. Antibiotics come later; they are not the first-aid step.
## answer_d
Patch the eye and refer to emergency hospital.
## explanation_d
Incorrect. Patching traps the chemical against the eye and worsens the injury.

Risk of a metallic intraocular foreign body
## question
Intra ocular foreign body (IOFB) with metallic nature carries the risk of:
## subject
mul
## status
Draft
## owner
Claude
## vignette

## correct_answer
A
## answer_a
Endophthalmitis and chemical reaction.
## explanation_a
Correct. A metallic intraocular foreign body risks endophthalmitis and a chemical (metallosis) reaction — siderosis from iron, chalcosis from copper.

Esotropia.
## explanation_b
Incorrect. A convergent squint is not a consequence of an intraocular foreign body.

Ptosis.
## explanation_c
Incorrect. Drooping of the lid is not caused by an intraocular foreign body.
## answer_d
Lacrimal sac swelling.
## explanation_d
Incorrect. Lacrimal sac swelling reflects tear-duct disease, not an intraocular foreign body.

## title
Effect of a blunt blow to the eye
## question
Blunt trauma to the eye with a fist can cause:
## subject
mul
## status
Draft
## owner
Claude
## vignette

## correct_answer
A
## answer_a
Subluxation of the lens.
## explanation_a
Correct. A blunt blow can rupture the zonular fibres and subluxate (or dislocate) the lens.
## answer_b
Acute angle closure glaucoma.
## explanation_b
Incorrect. Acute angle-closure glaucoma arises from a predisposed narrow angle, not directly from a blunt blow.
## answer_c
Tractional retinal detachment.
## explanation_c
Incorrect. Traction detachment follows fibrovascular proliferation (e.g. diabetes), not an acute blunt blow.
## answer_d
Fracture of the lateral wall of the orbit.
## explanation_d
Incorrect. Blunt trauma classically fractures the weak orbital floor or medial wall (blow-out), not the strong lateral wall.

## title
Management of a penetrating corneal laceration
## question
In a case of penetrating corneal laceration you should:
## subject
mul
## status
Draft
## owner
Claude
## vignette

## correct_answer
B
## answer_a
Put antibiotic eye drops frequently until surgical repair is done.
## explanation_a
Incorrect. Frequent drops do not substitute for the urgent surgical repair an open globe needs.
## answer_b
Patch the eye after examination, give systemic antibiotic and prepare for surgical repair.
## explanation_b
Correct. An open globe is gently shielded after examination, covered with systemic antibiotics, and prepared for surgical repair.
## answer_c
Do ultrasound urgently to exclude IOFB.
## explanation_c
Incorrect. Contact ultrasound presses on an open globe and risks extruding its contents.
## answer_d
Wash with betadine and prepare for surgery.
## explanation_d
Incorrect. Irrigating an open wound with betadine can drive it into the eye; it is not the correct step.
