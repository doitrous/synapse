Most important treatment of traumatic hyphema
## question

## subject
mul
## status

B

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Antibiotics do not address the sight-threatening rise in intraocular pressure that complicates hyphema.

## title
Effect of a blunt blow to the eye
## question
Blunt trauma to the eye with a fist can cause:
## subject
mul
## status
Draft
## owner
Claude
## vignette

## correct_answer
A
## answer_a
Subluxation of the lens.
## explanation_a
Correct. A blunt blow can rupture the zonular fibres and subluxate (or dislocate) the lens.
## answer_b
Acute angle closure glaucoma.
## explanation_b
Incorrect. Acute angle-closure glaucoma arises from a predisposed narrow angle, not directly from a blunt blow.
## answer_c
Tractional retinal detachment.
## explanation_c
Incorrect. Traction detachment follows fibrovascular proliferation (e.g. diabetes), not an acute blunt blow.
## answer_d
Fracture of the lateral wall of the orbit.
## explanation_d
Incorrect. Blunt trauma classically fractures the weak orbital floor or medial wall (blow-out), not the strong lateral wall.
