## explanation_a
Incorrect. Not all antivirals are night ointments; several are given as day-time drops.

Incorrect. Their duration of use is not fixed at two weeks.

## explanation_a
Incorrect (a genuine use). Fluorescein stains the epithelial defect of a corneal ulcer.
