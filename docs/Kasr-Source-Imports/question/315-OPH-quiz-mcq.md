## title
Features of Horner's syndrome
## question
Horner's syndrome is characterized by:
## subject
mul
## status

## owner

## vignette

## correct_answer
D
## answer_a
Miosis.
## explanation_a
Incorrect as the single best answer. Miosis (a small pupil from loss of sympathetic dilator tone) is a feature of Horner's syndrome, but it is only one component and not the complete answer.
## answer_b
Anhidrosis.
## explanation_b
Incorrect as the single best answer. Anhidrosis (loss of sweating over the affected side, when the lesion is proximal) is one component of the syndrome, not the whole picture.
## answer_c
Ptosis.
## explanation_c
Incorrect as the single best answer. The partial ptosis from paralysis of the sympathetically innervated Muller's muscle is one sign, but not the complete answer.
## answer_d
All of the above.
## explanation_d
Correct. Horner's syndrome results from interruption of the ocular sympathetic pathway and is characterized by the triad of miosis, partial ptosis (Muller's muscle) and anhidrosis, with apparent enophthalmos. All three listed features are present, so all of the above is the single best answer.
