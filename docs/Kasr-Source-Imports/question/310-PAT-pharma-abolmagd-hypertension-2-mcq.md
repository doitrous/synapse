## title
Antihypertensive whose adverse effect is tachycardia

## question
Tachycardia is one of the adverse effects of which of the following antihypertensive drugs?

## subject
pharm

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
D

## answer_a
Verapamil

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Verapamil, a non-dihydropyridine calcium channel blocker, slows the sinoatrial and AV nodes and tends to cause bradycardia, not tachycardia.

## answer_b
Nadolol

## explanation_b
Incorrect. Nadolol is a non-selective beta blocker; it lowers heart rate.

## answer_c
Captopril

## explanation_c
Incorrect. ACE inhibitors lower blood pressure without a marked reflex tachycardia and are not characterised by this effect.

## answer_d
Nifedipine

## explanation_d
Correct. Nifedipine is a dihydropyridine calcium channel blocker that is a potent arteriolar vasodilator with little direct cardiac effect. The fall in blood pressure activates the baroreceptor reflex, producing REFLEX TACHYCARDIA (and palpitations). This sympathetic reflex is the characteristic adverse effect of dihydropyridines, so nifedipine is the answer.

## title
Contraindication to nitrates in angina

## question
Nitrates used in the treatment of angina are contraindicated in combination with:

## subject
pharm

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
D

## answer_a
Variant angina

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Nitrates relieve coronary spasm and are useful, not contraindicated, in variant (vasospastic) angina.

## answer_b
Congestive heart failure

## explanation_b
Incorrect. Nitrates reduce preload and are used in congestive heart failure (e.g. the hydralazine-nitrate regimen).

## answer_c
Acute myocardial infarction

## explanation_c
Incorrect. Nitrates are given (by infusion) in acute myocardial infarction for ongoing ischaemia and pulmonary congestion, with caution for hypotension.

## answer_d
In combination with sildenafil

## explanation_d
Correct. Nitrates raise cGMP by donating nitric oxide, and PDE5 inhibitors such as sildenafil prevent cGMP breakdown. Given together they cause profound, potentially fatal HYPOTENSION, so the combination is contraindicated. Concurrent PDE5-inhibitor use is the classic nitrate contraindication tested here.

## title
Mechanism of the antihypertensive effect of ACE inhibitors

## question
Which of the following is one of the mechanisms of the antihypertensive effect of angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors (ACEIs)?

## subject
pharm

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
B

## answer_a
Stimulating D1 receptors

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Dopamine-1 receptor stimulation is the mechanism of fenoldopam, not of ACE inhibitors.

## answer_b
Decreasing aldosterone secretion

## explanation_b
Correct. ACE inhibitors block conversion of angiotensin I to angiotensin II. Less angiotensin II means less direct vasoconstriction AND less stimulation of adrenal aldosterone release, so DECREASED ALDOSTERONE reduces sodium and water retention and lowers blood pressure. Reduced aldosterone secretion is therefore a genuine antihypertensive mechanism of ACE inhibitors.

## answer_c
Blocking angiotensin II type 1 receptors

## explanation_c
Incorrect. Blocking AT1 receptors is the mechanism of angiotensin receptor blockers (ARBs); ACE inhibitors reduce angiotensin II formation upstream.

## answer_d
Blocking alpha-adrenergic receptors

## explanation_d
Incorrect. Alpha-adrenergic blockade is the mechanism of prazosin, not of ACE inhibitors.

## title
Comorbidity that contraindicates non-selective beta-blockers

## question
A patient with hypertension should avoid non-selective beta-blockers if he also suffers from:

## subject
pharm

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
A

## answer_a
Bronchial asthma

## explanation_a
Correct. Non-selective beta blockers antagonise beta-2 receptors in bronchial smooth muscle, removing sympathetic bronchodilation and precipitating BRONCHOSPASM. In a patient with bronchial asthma this can cause a severe, even fatal, attack, so non-selective beta blockers are contraindicated. Airway disease is the classic reason to avoid them.

## answer_b
Senile prostatic hyperplasia

## explanation_b
Incorrect. Benign prostatic hyperplasia is treated with alpha-1 blockers; it is not a contraindication to beta blockers.

## answer_c
Angina pectoris

## explanation_c
Incorrect. Beta blockers are beneficial in exertional angina, reducing myocardial oxygen demand.

## answer_d
Glaucoma

## explanation_d
Incorrect. Beta blockers (e.g. timolol) are actually used to lower intraocular pressure in glaucoma.

## title
Mechanism of beta-blockers in treating hypertension

## question
Which of the following is the mechanism of action of beta-blockers in the treatment of hypertension?

## subject
pharm

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
B

## answer_a
Peripheral vasodilatation

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Standard beta blockers do not cause peripheral vasodilatation; non-selective agents can even leave alpha-mediated vasoconstriction unopposed early on.

## answer_b
Decreasing of cardiac output

## explanation_b
Correct. Beta-1 blockade reduces heart rate and myocardial contractility, so CARDIAC OUTPUT falls, which lowers blood pressure. Beta blockers also reduce renin release from the kidney, but the primary haemodynamic mechanism among these options is the reduction in cardiac output.

## answer_c
Increase release of renin

## explanation_c
Incorrect. Beta blockers DECREASE renin release (beta-1 receptors on juxtaglomerular cells); they do not increase it.

## answer_d
Stimulation of imidazoline receptors

## explanation_d
Incorrect. Imidazoline receptor stimulation is the mechanism of centrally acting agents such as moxonidine, not beta blockers.

## title
Antihypertensive drug incorrectly paired with its adverse effect

## question
The following antihypertensive drug is NOT correctly paired with its adverse effect:

## subject
pharm

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
C

## answer_a
Captopril — Dry cough

## explanation_a
Incorrect (this pairing is correct). ACE inhibitors raise bradykinin, which commonly causes a dry cough, so captopril is correctly paired.

## answer_b
Minoxidil — Hypertrichosis

## explanation_b
Incorrect (this pairing is correct). Minoxidil characteristically causes hypertrichosis (excess hair growth), so the pairing is correct.

## answer_c
Diazoxide — Hypoglycaemia

## explanation_c
Correct. This is the MISMATCHED pair. Diazoxide opens ATP-sensitive potassium channels in pancreatic beta cells and inhibits insulin release, so it causes HYPERglycaemia, not hypoglycaemia. Because the stem asks for the incorrect pairing, diazoxide-hypoglycaemia is the answer.

## answer_d
Verapamil — Constipation

## explanation_d
Incorrect (this pairing is correct). Verapamil frequently causes constipation through smooth-muscle calcium blockade, so the pairing is correct.

## title
Action shared by ACE inhibitors and ARBs except one

## question
Angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors and angiotensin receptor blockers share the following actions EXCEPT:

## subject
pharm

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
A

## answer_a
Precipitate cough attacks in susceptible patients

## explanation_a
Correct. This is the EXCEPTION. The dry cough is caused by accumulation of bradykinin, which ACE normally degrades; ACE inhibitors block that degradation and provoke cough, whereas ARBs act at the AT1 receptor and do NOT raise bradykinin, so they do not typically cause cough. Cough is therefore the action NOT shared with ARBs.

## answer_b
Prevent post-myocardial infarction remodeling

## explanation_b
Incorrect (this action is shared). Both ACE inhibitors and ARBs reduce angiotensin II effects and limit adverse post-infarction remodeling.

## answer_c
Prevent progression of compensated to decompensated heart failure

## explanation_c
Incorrect (this action is shared). Both classes are used to slow heart-failure progression.

## answer_d
Can be used in hypertensive diabetic patients

## explanation_d
Incorrect (this action is shared). Both are renoprotective and preferred in hypertensive diabetics.
