Laser for corneal refractive surgery
## question
Which LASER is used for corneal refractive surgery?
## subject
mul
## status
Draft
## owner

## vignette

## correct_answer
C
## answer_a
Diode laser.
## explanation_a
Incorrect. The diode laser is used for retinal/cyclophotocoagulation, not corneal refractive surgery.
## answer_b
Carbon dioxide laser.
## explanation_b
Incorrect. The CO2 laser is used for tissue cutting/coagulation in oculoplastics, not corneal ablation.
## answer_c
Excimer laser.
## explanation_c
Correct. The excimer laser reshapes the cornea in refractive surgery (LASIK/PRK).
## answer_d
ND YAG laser.
## explanation_d
Incorrect. The Nd:YAG laser is used for capsulotomy and iridotomy, not refractive ablation.
