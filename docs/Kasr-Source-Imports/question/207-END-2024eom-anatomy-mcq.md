## title
Posterior relations of the rectum

## question
The rectum is related posteriorly to:

## subject
gi

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
A

## answer_a
Median sacral artery

## explanation_a
Correct. The posterior relations of the rectum are the lower three sacral and the coccygeal vertebrae, the median sacral vessels, the sympathetic chains, the lower sacral nerves, and the piriformis, coccygeus and levator ani muscles with the anococcygeal body. The median sacral artery descends on the front of the sacrum and coccyx directly behind the rectum.

## answer_b
Upper 1/3 of posterior wall of vagina in female

## explanation_b
Incorrect. The vagina lies in front of the rectum; the posterior vaginal wall is an anterior relation of the rectum in the female, separated by the rectovaginal septum.

## answer_c
Ductus deferens in male

## explanation_c
Incorrect. The ampulla of the ductus deferens lies on the base of the bladder, which is an anterior relation of the rectum in the male.

## answer_d
Rectovesical pouch

## explanation_d
Incorrect. The rectovesical pouch of peritoneum lies in front of the rectum between it and the bladder, so it is an anterior relation.
