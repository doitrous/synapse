## title
Sinus in the inferior border of the falx cerebri

## question
Which dural venous sinus is located at the inferior (free) border of the falx cerebri?

## subject
neuro

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
A

## answer_a
Inferior sagittal

## explanation_a
Correct. The inferior sagittal sinus runs in the free (lower, concave) border of the falx cerebri, above the corpus callosum, and drains backward into the straight sinus. The superior sagittal sinus, by contrast, occupies the attached (upper) border.

## answer_b
Straight

## explanation_b
Incorrect. The straight sinus lies along the line of attachment of the falx cerebri to the tentorium cerebelli, not in the free border of the falx; it receives the inferior sagittal sinus and the great cerebral vein.

## answer_c
Occipital

## explanation_c
Incorrect. The occipital sinus lies in the attached margin of the falx cerebelli, in the posterior cranial fossa, not in the falx cerebri.

## answer_d
Transverse

## explanation_d
Incorrect. The transverse sinus runs in the attached (posterolateral) margin of the tentorium cerebelli, draining toward the sigmoid sinus.

Incorrect. The glossopharyngeal nerve (CN IX) carries taste and general sensation from the POSTERIOR one-third of the tongue, not the anterior two-thirds.

Muscle that retracts the protruded mandible

Which muscle returns the mandibular condyle to its normal position after the mandible has been protracted?

Structure passing deep to hyoglossus

## question
Which structure passes deep to the hyoglossus muscle?

## subject
neuro

Draft

Claude

## correct_answer
A

## answer_a
Glossopharyngeal nerve

## explanation_a
Correct. The glossopharyngeal nerve passes deep (medial) to hyoglossus, together with the lingual artery and the stylohyoid ligament. This deep (medial) relationship is why the nerve is reached from the medial side of the muscle in the floor of the mouth.
