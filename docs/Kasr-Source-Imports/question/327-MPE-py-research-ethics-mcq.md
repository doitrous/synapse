## title
Vulnerable groups in medical research

## question
Vulnerable groups in medical research include those who are:

## subject
mul

## status

## owner

## vignette

## correct_answer

## answer_a
Cancer patients

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Having cancer does not itself impair the capacity to give free, informed consent; a diagnosis alone does not make a participant a vulnerable group in the consent-capacity sense.

## answer_b
Immobile

## explanation_b
Incorrect. Being immobile is a physical limitation that does not, by itself, impair decision-making capacity or the ability to consent to research.

## answer_c
Mentally ill

## explanation_c
Correct. The mentally ill are a vulnerable group in medical research because their illness can impair the capacity to give free and informed consent, so they need special protection against exploitation and coercion. Vulnerability here is about compromised autonomy and the ability to consent, not physical illness. This is why mentally ill participants, not the other groups listed, are classed as vulnerable.

## answer_d
Terminally ill

## explanation_d
Incorrect. A terminal illness does not necessarily remove decision-making capacity; being terminally ill does not by itself make a competent adult a vulnerable group in this sense.
