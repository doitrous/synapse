Artery ligated when reducing a hernia
## question
In order to reduce a hernia, a surgeon finds it necessary to ligate an artery in the extraperitoneal connective tissue running vertically just medial to the bowel. This artery is the:

gi

B
## answer_a

## explanation_a
Incorrect. The deep circumflex iliac artery runs laterally toward the anterior superior iliac spine, not vertically medial to the bowel at this site.
