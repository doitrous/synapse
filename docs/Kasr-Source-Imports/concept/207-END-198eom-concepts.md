## label
The gonad remains indifferent until about the 7th week
## canonical_key
gonad.indifferent-stage.differentiation-begins-7th-week
## aliases
Indifferent gonad stage
Undifferentiated gonad
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
The gonad develops from the genital ridge as a sexually undifferentiated (indifferent) organ with both a cortex and a medulla. It stays morphologically identical in both sexes until about the 7th week, when SRY (with a Y chromosome) directs the medulla to form a testis, or in its absence the cortex forms an ovary. Before then the gonad cannot be sexed histologically.
## explicit_objective
State that the gonad is indifferent until about the 7th week, when sexual differentiation begins.
## pitfalls
Assuming gonadal sex is fixed at fertilisation; the indifferent stage persists to about week 7.
