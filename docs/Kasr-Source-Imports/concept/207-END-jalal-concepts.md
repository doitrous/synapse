## label
The prostatic utricle and urethral crest lie on the posterior wall of the prostatic urethra
## canonical_key
prostatic-utricle.opening.posterior-wall-urethral-crest
## aliases
Prostatic utricle opening
Urethral crest and colliculus seminalis
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
The prostatic urethra has a median ridge, the urethral crest, running down the middle of its POSTERIOR wall. On the summit of the crest is the colliculus seminalis (seminal colliculus), where the blind prostatic utricle opens in the midline and the two ejaculatory ducts open on either side. The anterior wall of the prostatic urethra is featureless, so a stem placing the utricle opening on the anterior wall is false.
## explicit_objective
State that the prostatic utricle opens on the urethral crest on the posterior wall of the prostatic urethra.
## pitfalls
Placing the utricle opening or urethral crest on the anterior wall rather than the posterior wall.

## label
The bulbourethral (Cowper) gland duct opens into the spongy (penile) urethra
## canonical_key
bulbourethral-gland.duct.opens-into-spongy-urethra
## aliases
Cowper gland duct opening
Bulbourethral duct into spongy urethra
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
The two bulbourethral (Cowper) glands lie in the deep perineal pouch on either side of the membranous urethra, but each has a long duct that pierces the perineal membrane and opens into the proximal part of the SPONGY (penile) urethra within the bulb of the penis. The gland is therefore membranous in position but its duct opens into the spongy urethra. Its mucous secretion lubricates and neutralises acidic urine in the urethra before ejaculation.
## explicit_objective
State that the bulbourethral (Cowper) gland duct opens into the spongy (penile) urethra.
## pitfalls
Assuming the duct opens where the gland lies (deep perineal pouch / membranous urethra) rather than more distally in the spongy urethra.

The prostatic urethra is the widest and most dilatable part of the male urethra
## canonical_key
prostatic-urethra.feature.widest-most-dilatable-part

## arabic_aliases

## definition
Of the three parts of the male urethra, the prostatic urethra (about 3 cm) is the widest and most dilatable. The membranous urethra is the shortest, narrowest and least dilatable part, and the external urethral orifice at the meatus is the narrowest fixed point overall. Knowing this order matters for the safe passage of instruments and catheters.
## explicit_objective
Rank the prostatic urethra as the widest and most dilatable part of the male urethra.
## pitfalls
Assuming the widest lumen coincides with the narrowest fixed point; the prostatic part is widest while the meatus is narrowest.

The external urethral sphincter is supplied by the pudendal nerve
## canonical_key
external-urethral-sphincter.innervation.pudendal-nerve

## arabic_label

## definition
The external urethral sphincter (sphincter urethrae) is voluntary skeletal muscle in the deep perineal pouch surrounding the membranous urethra.
