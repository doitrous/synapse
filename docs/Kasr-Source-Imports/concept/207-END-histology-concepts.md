CON-END-BCB509D67E652C
## label
Herring bodies are accumulations of neurosecretory material in the axon terminals of the pars nervosa
## canonical_key
pituitary.pars-nervosa.herring-bodies
## aliases
Herring bodies of the pars nervosa
pituitary neurosecretion axon terminals
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
Herring bodies are dilated swellings along the unmyelinated axons of the pars nervosa in which oxytocin and ADH (bound to neurophysin) accumulate before release. The hormones are made in the hypothalamic supraoptic and paraventricular nuclei and transported down the hypothalamo-hypophyseal tract. They appear as pale eosinophilic bodies among the pituicytes.
## explicit_objective
Identify Herring bodies as neurosecretory stores in pars nervosa axon terminals.
## pitfalls
Confusing Herring bodies with cell bodies; the pars nervosa has almost no neuronal somata, only axons, terminals and pituicytes.

## label
The basal zone (stratum basalis) is the retained reserve layer that regenerates the endometrium
## canonical_key
endometrium.basal-zone.reserve-regeneration
## aliases
Basal zone of the endometrium
stratum basalis functionalis spiral straight artery
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
The endometrium has a superficial functional zone (shed at menstruation, supplied by spiral arteries) and a deep basal zone (stratum basalis) supplied by its own straight arteries. The basalis is retained during menstruation and acts as the regenerative reserve from which the functional layer is rebuilt each proliferative phase. The lining is simple columnar with simple tubular glands.
## explicit_objective
Identify the stratum basalis as the retained reserve layer regenerating the endometrium.
## pitfalls
Assigning spiral arteries or shedding to the basal zone; those belong to the functional zone.

## definition
Under oestrogen, the non-keratinised stratified squamous vaginal epithelium accumulates glycogen; commensal lactobacilli (Doderlein bacilli) ferment it into lactic acid, giving a protective acidic pH (~4) that inhibits pathogens. The vagina has no glands and is lubricated by cervical mucus and transudate; its wall is rich in elastic fibres.
## explicit_objective
Explain the glycogen-to-lactic-acid mechanism that acidifies the vaginal lumen.
## pitfalls
Thinking the vagina has mucous glands or lacks elastic fibres, or that it is simple columnar epithelium.

## arabic_label

## definition
The proliferative (follicular, oestrogenic) phase rebuilds the functional layer from the basalis; the regenerating endometrial glands are straight, narrow and non-secretory. They become coiled and dilated only in the secretory (luteal) phase. Spiral-artery constriction and shedding belong to the premenstrual/menstrual phases, not proliferation.
## explicit_objective
Recognise straight narrow glands as the hallmark of the proliferative phase.
## pitfalls
Confusing proliferative straight glands with secretory-phase coiled glands, or placing shedding here.

## label
The cytotrophoblast is a mononuclear layer with clear cell boundaries that fuses to form the syncytiotrophoblast
## canonical_key
placenta.cytotrophoblast.clear-cell-boundaries
## aliases
Cytotrophoblast of the placenta
Langhans syncytiotrophoblast trophoblast villus
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
The cytotrophoblast (of Langhans) is the inner trophoblast layer of discrete mononuclear stem cells with clear, well-defined boundaries and pale cytoplasm; they proliferate and fuse to form the outer multinucleated syncytiotrophoblast. The cytotrophoblast is prominent early and becomes discontinuous toward term, whereas clustered nuclei (syncytial knots) belong to the syncytium.
## explicit_objective
Distinguish the mononuclear cytotrophoblast from the multinucleated syncytiotrophoblast.
## pitfalls
Assigning clustered nuclei or the syncytial layer to the cytotrophoblast, or expecting it to persist as a full layer at term.

## aliases

## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
In the inactive (resting) mammary gland the parenchyma is mostly a duct system lined by simple cuboidal (to low columnar) epithelium surrounded by contractile myoepithelial cells between epithelium and basement membrane, set in abundant connective tissue and fat with thick interlobular septa. Secretory alveoli and milk appear only with pregnancy and lactation.
## explicit_objective
Describe the duct-lining cuboidal cells and myoepithelium of the resting mammary gland.
## pitfalls
Expecting active alveoli, milk or thin fat-poor septa in the resting gland; those are lactational features.
