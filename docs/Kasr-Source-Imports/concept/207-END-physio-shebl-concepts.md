## label
Pro-opiomelanocortin is cleaved in pituitary corticotrophs to yield ACTH
## canonical_key
pro-opiomelanocortin.precursor.acth
## aliases
POMC is the parent peptide of ACTH, MSH and beta-endorphin
ACTH is derived from pro-opiomelanocortin
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
Pro-opiomelanocortin (POMC) is a large precursor polypeptide synthesised by the corticotrophs of the anterior pituitary (and the pars intermedia). Enzymatic cleavage of POMC produces adrenocorticotropic hormone (ACTH) along with melanocyte-stimulating hormone, beta-lipotropin and beta-endorphin. Cortisol, growth hormone, prolactin and TSH are not POMC products: cortisol is an adrenal steroid, while GH, prolactin and TSH are transcribed from their own genes.
## explicit_objective
Identify ACTH as the anterior-pituitary hormone derived from cleavage of pro-opiomelanocortin.
## pitfalls
Confusing the precursor with the target-gland steroid it stimulates: POMC yields ACTH, whereas cortisol is the downstream adrenal steroid, not a POMC cleavage product.

## label
Growth hormone acts through the JAK2-STAT signalling pathway
## canonical_key
growth-hormone.signal-transduction.jak2-stat
## aliases
GH receptor is a cytokine-family receptor coupled to JAK2
Growth hormone signalling is mediated by Janus kinase 2 and STAT proteins
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
The growth-hormone receptor is a single-membrane-pass receptor of the cytokine-receptor family that has no intrinsic enzymatic activity. Growth hormone binds and dimerises the receptor, activating the associated Janus kinase 2 (JAK2). JAK2 phosphorylates the receptor and STAT transcription factors, which then dimerise and move to the nucleus to change gene transcription. This distinguishes GH from lipophilic thyroid hormones (nuclear receptors) and from vasopressin and glucagon (G-protein-coupled, second-messenger) signalling.
## explicit_objective
State that growth hormone signals through JAK2 activation and STAT phosphorylation.
## pitfalls
Assuming every peptide hormone uses cyclic AMP; the GH receptor recruits JAK2-STAT rather than generating a diffusible second messenger.

## definition
Somatomedins (insulin-like growth factors) are produced by the liver under growth-hormone drive and circulate almost entirely bound to specific IGF-binding proteins. Thinking somatomedins act briefly like a rapidly cleared peptide; their binding proteins give a long half-life, and they inhibit rather than stimulate GH.

Confusing the V2 renal water action with the V1 vascular action; only the V2 pathway inserts aquaporin-2 to reabsorb water.

## definition
Stretch of the cervix and upper vagina during labour generates afferent neural signals that reach the hypothalamic magnocellular neurons and trigger oxytocin release from the posterior pituitary. The released oxytocin increases uterine contraction, which dilates the cervix further and drives yet more oxytocin release - a positive-feedback loop known as the Ferguson reflex. Suckling is the separate stimulus for the milk-ejection reflex; a rise in prolactin or in extracellular fluid volume is not a stimulus for oxytocin secretion.
## explicit_objective
Identify cervical/vaginal stretch as the stimulus for oxytocin release in the Ferguson positive-feedback reflex.
## pitfalls
Mistaking a downstream effect (milk ejection, uterine contraction) for the stimulus; the trigger is cervical/vaginal stretch, not the effect it produces.

The NIS concentrates iodide using the sodium gradient

## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
Iodide is taken up into the thyroid follicular cell across the basolateral membrane by the sodium-iodide symporter (NIS). NIS carries iodide against its electrochemical gradient by coupling it to the inward movement of sodium down the gradient maintained by the Na-K ATPase, so it is a form of secondary active transport. This iodide-trapping step concentrates iodide many-fold within the gland and is stimulated by TSH.
## explicit_objective
Classify thyroid iodide trapping by the sodium-iodide symporter as secondary active transport.
## pitfalls
Calling the iodide pump primary active transport; the carrier does not hydrolyse ATP itself but uses the sodium gradient set up by the Na-K ATPase.

Most circulating T3 is made from T4 by type 1 deiodinase in peripheral tissues

thyroid-hormone.peripheral-conversion.type-1-deiodinase-t4-to-t3

## definition
The thyroid secretes mostly thyroxine (T4), and most of the more active triiodothyronine (T3) is generated peripherally by outer-ring (5'-) deiodination of T4. ## explicit_objective
Identify type 1 deiodinase as the enzyme generating most circulating T3 from peripheral T4.
## pitfalls
Assuming the thyroid secretes T3 directly in bulk; most active T3 is produced outside the gland by D1-mediated deiodination of T4.
