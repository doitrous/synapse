## label
The thyroid gland is drained by three paired veins (superior, middle and inferior thyroid veins)
## canonical_key
thyroid-gland.venous-drainage.three-paired-veins
## aliases
Superior, middle and inferior thyroid veins
Thyroid venous drainage
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
The thyroid gland is drained by three pairs of veins arising from a plexus on its surface. The superior and middle thyroid veins drain into the internal jugular vein, while the inferior thyroid veins descend in front of the trachea to reach the brachiocephalic veins. Thus three paired veins, not a single vein, carry its venous blood, and the pattern does not follow the arteries one-for-one.
## explicit_objective
State that the thyroid gland is drained by superior, middle and inferior thyroid veins.
## pitfalls
Assuming the veins simply mirror the two main arteries and missing the third (inferior) pair.

## label
The thyroid gland moves up and down with swallowing because the pretracheal fascia binds it to the larynx
## canonical_key
thyroid-gland.deglutition.moves-with-swallowing
## aliases
Thyroid mobility on swallowing
Ligament of Berry and deglutition
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
The thyroid gland is enclosed by the pretracheal fascia, which forms its false capsule and binds it to the larynx and trachea, notably through the posteromedial suspensory ligament of Berry. Because the larynx and trachea ascend during swallowing, the gland moves up and down with deglutition. This mobility on swallowing is the classic clinical sign that a midline neck swelling is thyroid in origin, distinguishing it from most other neck masses.
## explicit_objective
Explain why the thyroid gland (and thyroid swellings) move with swallowing.
## pitfalls
Attributing the movement to the investing layer of fascia rather than the pretracheal fascia and Berry ligament.

## label
The recurrent laryngeal nerve is the nerve injured in thyroidectomy that causes hoarseness
## canonical_key
recurrent-laryngeal-nerve.thyroidectomy-injury.hoarseness
## aliases
Recurrent laryngeal nerve palsy
Vocal cord paralysis after thyroidectomy
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
The recurrent laryngeal nerve runs in the tracheo-oesophageal groove behind the thyroid lobe, closely related to the inferior thyroid artery, and supplies all intrinsic muscles of the larynx except the cricothyroid. Injury during partial thyroidectomy paralyses the vocal cord and produces hoarseness of voice; bilateral injury threatens the airway. It is therefore the nerve classically responsible for post-thyroidectomy hoarseness, in contrast to the external laryngeal nerve which only weakens the cricothyroid.
## explicit_objective
Identify the recurrent laryngeal nerve as the cause of hoarseness after thyroidectomy.
## pitfalls
Confusing recurrent laryngeal (cord paralysis, hoarseness) with external laryngeal (cricothyroid weakness, pitch change).

## label
The splenic artery, not the splenic vein, runs along the upper border of the body of the pancreas
## canonical_key
pancreas.upper-border.splenic-artery-not-vein
## aliases
Splenic artery along pancreatic upper border
Splenic vein behind the body of pancreas
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
The splenic artery runs a characteristically tortuous course along the upper border of the body of the pancreas toward the spleen. The splenic vein, by contrast, lies behind the body of the pancreas, below the artery, and receives the inferior mesenteric vein before uniting with the superior mesenteric vein behind the neck to form the portal vein. It is therefore wrong to say the splenic vein lies along the upper border of the body.
## explicit_objective
Distinguish the splenic artery (upper border) from the splenic vein (behind the body).
## pitfalls
Placing the splenic vein along the upper border, which is the course of the splenic artery.

## label
The inferior thyroid veins are endangered by a low midline tracheostomy incision
## canonical_key
midline-tracheostomy.venous-injury.inferior-thyroid-veins
## aliases
Inferior thyroid venous plexus
Thyroidea ima vein in tracheostomy
## arabic_label

## definition
