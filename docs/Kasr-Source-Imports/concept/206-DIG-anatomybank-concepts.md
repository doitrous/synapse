## label
The rectum begins at the rectosigmoid junction in front of the S3 vertebra
## canonical_key
rectum.begins.rectosigmoid-junction-s3
## aliases
rectum start level S3
rectosigmoid junction level
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
The rectum begins at the rectosigmoid junction, opposite the third sacral vertebra (S3), where the sigmoid colon loses its mesentery. It follows the concavity of the sacrum and coccyx as the sacral flexure, then turns backwards at the anorectal junction to become the anal canal. Its upper third is covered by peritoneum on the front and sides, the middle third on the front only, and the lower third lies below the peritoneal reflection.
## explicit_objective
State that the rectum begins at the rectosigmoid junction opposite S3.
## pitfalls
Placing the origin at the sacral promontory (S1) rather than at S3.

The puborectalis is the most medial and inferior part of the levator ani. The pelvic part of the urogenital sinus forms the membranous urethra in the male

urogenital-sinus.pelvic-part.membranous-urethra

## definition
The urogenital sinus is divided into vesical, pelvic and phallic parts. ## definition
The oesophageal hiatus lies at the level of the tenth thoracic vertebra (T10) and is formed by fibres of the right crus of the diaphragm, which split and loop around the oesophagus as a muscular sling. State that the oesophageal hiatus (T10) is formed by the right crus of the diaphragm.
