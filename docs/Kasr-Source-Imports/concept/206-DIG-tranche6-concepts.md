## label
Hepatocytes are large polygonal cells with acidophilic, vacuolated cytoplasm and a pale nucleolated nucleus
## canonical_key
hepatocyte.cytology.acidophilic-vacuolated-cytoplasm
## aliases
Histological features of the hepatocyte
hepatocyte acidophilic vacuolated cytoplasm
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
Hepatocytes are large polygonal epithelial cells arranged in plates one-to-two cells thick. Their cytoplasm is predominantly acidophilic (abundant mitochondria and smooth ER) with basophilic patches (rough ER) and appears vacuolated because dissolved-out glycogen and lipid leave empty spaces. The nucleus is large, round, pale and euchromatic with one or more prominent nucleoli, and many hepatocytes are binucleate.
## explicit_objective
Recognise the hepatocyte by its polygonal shape, acidophilic vacuolated cytoplasm and pale nucleolated nucleus.
## pitfalls
Calling hepatocytes columnar, expecting long microvilli on the canalicular (rather than sinusoidal) surface, or expecting dark nuclei with indistinct nucleoli.

## definition
The ureter, renal pelvis, calyces and bladder share a urothelial (transitional epithelial) lining. Its superficial layer consists of large, often binucleate umbrella (dome) cells joined by tight junctions that create an impermeable barrier to hypertonic urine. The epithelium appears multilayered and rounded when relaxed and flattens on distension.
## explicit_objective
Match the ureter to a urothelial lining with surface umbrella cells.
## pitfalls
Confusing urothelium with stratified squamous epithelium, or forgetting the umbrella cells are the barrier layer.

## canonical_key

## aliases
Ultrastructure of the macula densa

## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
The macula densa is the plaque of tall, narrow distal-tubule cells that abuts the vascular pole of its own glomerulus. Its nuclei are numerous and crowded apically, giving the dense appearance, and its Golgi apparatus lies infranuclear (basal) rather than in the usual supranuclear position. It senses tubular NaCl and signals to the JG and mesangial cells to regulate GFR and renin release.
## explicit_objective
Identify the macula densa by its crowded apical nuclei and infranuclear Golgi.
## pitfalls
Confusing the macula densa with granular JG (pole cushion) cells, or expecting PAS-positive secretory granules within it.

## label
The glomerular basement membrane laminae rarae are negatively charged, giving the filtration barrier its charge selectivity
## canonical_key
glomerular-filtration-barrier.gbm.lamina-rara-negative-charge
## aliases
The glomerular (blood-renal) filtration barrier
GBM lamina rara negative charge
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
The three-layered glomerular filtration barrier is the fenestrated capillary endothelium, the glomerular basement membrane and the podocyte slit diaphragms. The GBM has a central lamina densa flanked by a lamina rara interna and externa rich in heparan sulfate proteoglycans, which carry a strong fixed negative charge. This charge repels anionic plasma proteins such as albumin, so filtration depends on both size and charge.
## explicit_objective
Describe the three-layered filtration barrier and the charge selectivity provided by the negatively charged laminae rarae.
## pitfalls
Thinking size is the only filtration parameter, that the glomerular endothelium is continuous, or that the slit diaphragm is the least effective layer.

PCT cells have acidophilic cytoplasm, an apical brush border, indistinct boundaries and basal infoldings enclosing parallel mitochondria
## canonical_key

## aliases
Histology of the proximal convoluted tubule
PCT basal infoldings parallel mitochondria
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
The proximal convoluted tubule is the nephron main resorptive segment, with a large diameter and a narrow, brush-border-filled lumen. Its cells are acidophilic (mitochondria-rich) with indistinct lateral boundaries from extensive interdigitations. Deep basal plasma-membrane infoldings enclose long mitochondria aligned vertically (parallel) to the basement membrane, housing the Na+/K+-ATPase that powers reabsorption.
## explicit_objective
Recognise the PCT by its acidophilic cytoplasm, brush border, indistinct boundaries and basal infoldings with parallel mitochondria.
## pitfalls
Expecting basophilic cytoplasm, clear cell boundaries, or a wide empty lumen in the PCT.

## label
Podocytes are the visceral epithelium whose actin-rich foot processes form the filtration slits
## canonical_key
podocyte.cytology.actin-microfilaments-foot-processes
## aliases
Structure of the podocyte
podocyte actin microfilaments foot processes
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
Podocytes form the visceral layer of Bowman capsule, wrapping the glomerular capillaries. Each sends primary processes that branch into numerous foot processes (pedicels) interdigitating over the GBM, with filtration slits bridged by slit diaphragms between them. The pedicels are packed with contractile bundles of actin microfilaments that adjust slit width and resist glomerular pressure.
## explicit_objective
Describe the podocyte as the visceral epithelium whose actin-rich foot processes form the slit diaphragms.
## pitfalls
Placing podocytes in the parietal layer, giving them only one major and one minor process, or confusing them with Sertoli cells of the blood-testis barrier.

## definition
The superficial umbrella cells of the bladder urothelium are joined by extensive intercellular junctional complexes, especially tight junctions, and carry apical uroplakin plaques that make the surface impermeable to urine. ## pitfalls
Thinking the empty-bladder urothelium is only 2-3 layers, or describing the detrusor as inner/outer circular with a middle longitudinal layer.
