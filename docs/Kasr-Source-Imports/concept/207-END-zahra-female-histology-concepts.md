## label
The zona pellucida is a glycoprotein coat formed by the oocyte and granulosa cells
## canonical_key
zona-pellucida.composition.glycoprotein-formed-by-oocyte-and-granulosa
## aliases
Zona pellucida is a glycoprotein layer around the oocyte
The zona pellucida is made by both the oocyte and follicular cells
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
The zona pellucida is a thick, refractile extracellular coat of glycoproteins (ZP1-3) lying between the oocyte and the surrounding follicular cells. It is synthesised by both the oocyte and the granulosa cells and is traversed by microvilli of the oocyte and cytoplasmic processes of the corona radiata. It first appears in the unilaminar primary follicle and is essential for sperm binding and the block to polyspermy at fertilisation.
## explicit_objective
State that the zona pellucida is a glycoprotein coat formed by the oocyte and granulosa cells.
## pitfalls
Attributing the zona pellucida to the theca; it is made by the oocyte and granulosa cells and is glycoprotein, not collagen.

## definition
A primordial follicle is a primary oocyte, arrested in prophase of the first meiotic division, surrounded by a single layer of flattened (squamous) follicular cells; these are the only follicles present in the ovary before puberty. Distinguish the flat follicular cells of the primordial follicle from the cuboidal layer of the primary follicle.
## pitfalls
Calling the primordial follicular cells cuboidal; they are flat, and cuboidal cells define the primary follicle.

## definition
The innermost follicular cells (corona radiata) extend cytoplasmic processes across the zona pellucida to meet microvilli projecting from the oocyte. Where they meet they form gap (communicating) junctions, which let ions, metabolites and signalling molecules pass directly between the follicular cells and the oocyte. This coupling nourishes the oocyte and helps hold it arrested in meiosis until the LH surge; the junction is a gap junction, not a tight, desmosome or adherens junction.
## explicit_objective
Identify the corona radiata-oocyte contact as a gap junction.
## pitfalls
Calling the corona-oocyte contact a desmosome or tight junction; it is a gap (communicating) junction.

## definition
The primary oocyte is arrested in prophase of the first meiotic division from fetal life until, just before ovulation, the LH surge allows it to complete meiosis I, producing a secondary oocyte and the first polar body. The secondary oocyte then arrests in metaphase of the second meiotic division and is ovulated; it completes meiosis II only if a spermatozoon fertilises it. Thus the first meiotic division is completed just before ovulation and the second only after fertilisation.
## explicit_objective
State that meiosis I completes just before ovulation and meiosis II only after fertilisation.
## pitfalls
Believing meiosis II completes at ovulation; it finishes only if fertilisation occurs.
