Thymus-independent antigens activate B cells without T-cell help
## canonical_key
immunology.thymus-independent-antigens-activate-b-cells-directly
## aliases

## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
Thymus-independent (TI) antigens - typically large repetitive polymers such as polysaccharides - can activate B cells directly, with the antigen alone delivering the signal and no requirement for T-helper cells; the response is predominantly IgM.
## explicit_objective
State that B-cell activation by thymus-independent antigens is delivered by the antigen alone.
## pitfalls
Because there is no T-cell help, TI responses do NOT produce isotype (class) switching or memory cells and the antibody response is not delayed; the defining feature is direct activation by the antigen only.
