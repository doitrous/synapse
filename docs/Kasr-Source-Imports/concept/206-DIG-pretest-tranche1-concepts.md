# Item
## id
CON-GIT-E9080094FC4F68
## label
Gastric emptying is slowed by duodenal feedback (fat, hyperosmolarity, acid), and liquids empty before solids
## canonical_key
stomach.gastric-emptying.duodenal-feedback-slows-emptying
## aliases
control of gastric emptying
enterogastric reflex slows emptying
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
The rate at which the stomach empties is set mainly by inhibitory feedback from the proximal small bowel rather than by gastric drive alone. Fat, a hyperosmolar load, and acid in the duodenum each slow emptying, largely through the neural enterogastric reflex sensed by duodenal osmoreceptors and chemoreceptors; emptying is therefore fastest for a carbohydrate meal and slowest after a fatty meal. Because solids must first be liquefied by trituration, liquids begin to empty before solids. Acid in the antrum inhibits gastrin and reduces motility, and the vagus mediates receptive relaxation so that the fundus accommodates a meal. In diabetic gastroparesis this inhibitory feedback is exaggerated relative to gastric contraction, delaying emptying and predisposing to reflux.
## explicit_objective
Explain that gastric emptying is slowed by duodenal feedback (fat, hyperosmolarity and acid), that liquids empty before solids, and that the vagus mediates receptive relaxation.
## pitfalls
Assuming solids empty before liquids or that fat empties faster than carbohydrate; or thinking vagal stimulation reduces receptive relaxation (it mediates it).

# Item
## id
CON-GIT-4ADDF23A15AC56
## label
Small-intestinal contractions are initiated by wall distension; the basal electrical rhythm falls aborally and MMCs sweep during fasting
## canonical_key
small-intestine.motility.distension-initiates-contraction-ber-mmc
## aliases
small intestinal motility
basal electrical rhythm and migrating motor complex
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
Small-intestinal contractile activity is triggered by distension of the bowel wall. The interstitial cells of Cajal generate the basal electrical rhythm, spontaneous membrane-potential oscillations that rarely cause contraction on their own but time it: contractions occur only on the depolarising phase, which coordinates peristalsis, segmentation and tonic contractions. The rhythm declines aborally, from roughly 12 cycles/min in the duodenum to about 8/min in the distal ileum. During fasting, migrating motor complexes sweep residue from stomach to ileum; they stop immediately when food is eaten. Vagotomy makes the pattern irregular and chaotic but does not abolish it, because the enteric nervous system and the cells of Cajal remain.
## explicit_objective
State that small-intestinal contractile activity is initiated by wall distension, that the basal electrical rhythm declines aborally, and that migrating motor complexes are a fasting pattern abolished by feeding.
## pitfalls
Believing the contractile frequency is uniform along the small bowel, that MMCs occur during digestion, or that vagotomy abolishes intestinal contractions.

# Item
## id
CON-GIT-3BC52EBDFA9CD1
## label
The terminal ileum actively reabsorbs bile salts, so its resection causes bile-salt malabsorption and steatorrhoea
## canonical_key
terminal-ileum.absorption.bile-salts-active-transport
## aliases
enterohepatic circulation of bile salts
bile salt absorption terminal ileum
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
The terminal ileum carries specialised active transporters that reabsorb conjugated bile salts and return them to the liver in the enterohepatic circulation. Resecting or diseasing the terminal ileum (as in Crohn's disease) prevents bile-salt reabsorption: the bile-salt pool falls, fat digestion and absorption suffer, and steatorrhoea results. Unabsorbed bile acids that spill into the colon raise mucosal adenylate cyclase activity and drive water secretion, adding a secretory diarrhoea. Distal small-bowel resection therefore causes greater malabsorption than removing the same length of proximal bowel. Nutrients absorbed proximally -- iron and folate in the duodenum/jejunum, lactose by brush-border lactase, and protein as amino acids and peptides -- are spared.
## explicit_objective
State that the terminal ileum actively reabsorbs bile salts, so its resection causes bile-salt malabsorption and steatorrhoea while proximally absorbed nutrients are spared.
## pitfalls
Attributing post-ileal-resection malabsorption to iron, folate, lactose or protein, which are absorbed proximally and spared.
