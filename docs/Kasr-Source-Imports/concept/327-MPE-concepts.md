## canonical_key

## definition
The medical profession in Egypt is regulated by both general laws that apply to everyone (such as the Penal Code) and special laws written specifically for medical practice (such as Law No. 415/1954 and the Profession Ethics Regulations 238/2003); the Penal Code is a general law.
## explicit_objective
Classify the Penal Code as a general law regulating the medical profession, distinct from the special laws of medical practice.
## pitfalls
Law No. 415/1954 and the Profession Ethics Regulations 238/2003 are special (profession-specific) laws, not general laws; do not label them "general".

When a physician takes proper informed consent and discloses the high risk of a procedure to the patient and relatives, a death or bad outcome from that recognised, consented risk is not actionable: valid informed consent is a legal defence because the patient accepted the known risk, and without a breach of duty a suit cannot succeed.
