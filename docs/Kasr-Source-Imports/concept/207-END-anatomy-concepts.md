CON-GIT-94E6662CB82128
## label
The median sacral artery is a posterior relation of the rectum
## canonical_key
rectum.posterior-relation.median-sacral-artery
## aliases
Posterior relations of the rectum
Median sacral vessels behind the rectum
## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
The rectum is related posteriorly to the lower three sacral and the coccygeal vertebrae, the median sacral vessels, the sympathetic chains, the lower sacral nerves and the piriformis, coccygeus and levator ani muscles. The median sacral artery descends on the front of the sacrum and coccyx directly behind the rectum. Structures such as the vagina, ductus deferens and rectovesical pouch are anterior, not posterior, relations.
## explicit_objective
List the posterior relations of the rectum, including the median sacral artery.
## pitfalls
Confusing anterior relations (vagina, ductus deferens, rectovesical pouch) with the posterior ones.
