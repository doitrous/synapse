## label
Brugia malayi adults live in the lymphatics

## canonical_key
brugia-malayi.habitat.lymphatics

## aliases


## arabic_label


## arabic_aliases


## definition
Brugia malayi is a lymphatic filarial worm whose adult worms live in the lymphatics and lymph nodes, where chronic infection causes lymphatic obstruction and lymphoedema similar to that caused by Wuchereria bancrofti; this distinguishes it from Onchocerca volvulus, which lives in subcutaneous nodules, and from Loa loa, whose adults migrate through the subcutaneous and connective tissues.

## explicit_objective
Identify the lymphatics as the habitat of adult Brugia malayi, alongside Wuchereria bancrofti, unlike the subcutaneous-dwelling Onchocerca volvulus and Loa loa.

## pitfalls
Assuming all filarial worms share the same tissue habitat; only Wuchereria bancrofti and Brugia malayi are lymphatic dwellers.
