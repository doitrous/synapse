## title
Neuroblastoma is an embryonic tumour

## question
Neuroblastoma is a/an:

## subject
fnd

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
C

## answer_a
Epithelial tumour

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Neuroblastoma is not an epithelial tumour; it does not arise from surface or glandular epithelium.

## answer_b
Mesenchymal tumour

## explanation_b
Incorrect. Neuroblastoma is not classified with the ordinary mesenchymal tumours (of fat, muscle, bone, cartilage); it belongs to a distinct family defined by embryonic-cell-remnant origin rather than mature mesenchymal tissue.

## answer_c
Embryonic tumor

## explanation_c
Correct. Neuroblastoma arises from primitive neural-crest-derived cells of the adrenal medulla and sympathetic ganglia in infants and young children. It is one of the department's five named embryonic tumours -- malignant tumours of primitive, undifferentiated cells derived from embryonic remnants -- alongside retinoblastoma, nephroblastoma, hepatoblastoma and medulloblastoma.

## answer_d
Germ cell tumour

## explanation_d
Incorrect. A germ cell tumour (e.g. teratoma, seminoma) arises from totipotent germ cells, a different lineage from the embryonic-remnant cells that give rise to neuroblastoma.

## question
The following tumour is NOT an embryonic tumour:

## subject
fnd

## status

## owner

## vignette

## answer_a
Retinoblastoma

## explanation_a
Incorrect (i.e. retinoblastoma IS an embryonic tumour). Retinoblastoma is one of the department's five named embryonic tumours, arising from primitive retinal cell remnants.

## answer_b
Medulloblastoma

## explanation_b
Incorrect (i.e. medulloblastoma IS an embryonic tumour). Medulloblastoma is one of the department's five named embryonic tumours, arising from primitive neuroectodermal remnants in the cerebellum.

## answer_c
Seminoma

## explanation_c
Correct (i.e. seminoma is NOT an embryonic tumour, the exception). Seminoma is a germ cell tumour, arising from totipotent germ cells of the testis, a different tumour family from the department's embryonic-tumour list of neuroblastoma, retinoblastoma, nephroblastoma, hepatoblastoma and medulloblastoma -- malignant tumours of primitive cells derived specifically from embryonic remnants, not germ cells.

## answer_d
Neuroblastoma

## explanation_d
Incorrect (i.e. neuroblastoma IS an embryonic tumour). Neuroblastoma is one of the department's five named embryonic tumours, arising from primitive neural-crest remnants.
