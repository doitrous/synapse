## answer_a
Teichoic acid

## explanation_a
Correct. This is the false statement, so it is the EXCEPT answer. Teichoic acid is a component threaded through the thick peptidoglycan wall of Gram-positive bacteria specifically; Gram-negative bacteria lack it entirely.

LPS

## explanation_b
Incorrect (genuine component, not the EXCEPT). Lipopolysaccharide (LPS) is carried on the Gram-negative outer membrane.

Peptidoglycan

Incorrect (genuine component, not the EXCEPT). The Gram-negative wall does contain peptidoglycan, though as a thin layer compared with the thick Gram-positive wall.

## answer_d
Outer membrane

## explanation_d
Incorrect (genuine component, not the EXCEPT). The outer membrane, carrying LPS and enclosing a periplasmic space, is a defining Gram-negative structure.

Incorrect. The chromosome carries the genetic template (DNA) that is transcribed into mRNA, but protein synthesis itself is physically carried out at the ribosome, not the chromosome.
