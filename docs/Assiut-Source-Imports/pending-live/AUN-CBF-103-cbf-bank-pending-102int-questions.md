## title
Total body water as a percentage of adult body weight

## question
The total water content in the body represents ……… of the total body weight of the normal adult

## subject
fnd

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
B

## answer_a
80%

## explanation_a
Incorrect. 80% is closer to the total body water fraction seen in infants, not the normal adult male value this question asks about.

## answer_b
60%

## explanation_b
Correct. Total body water (TBW) is about 60% of body weight in a normal adult male. It is the reference figure the rest of the compartment scheme (ICF 40%, ECF 20%) is built from. It is also the figure that shifts in special populations, higher in infants and lower in adult females, older adults and obese persons, all because of their different fat-to-lean-mass ratios.

## answer_c
40%

## explanation_c
Incorrect. 40% is the intracellular fluid (ICF) fraction of body weight, a subcompartment of total body water, not the TBW total itself.

## answer_d
20%

## explanation_d
Incorrect. 20% is the extracellular fluid (ECF) fraction of body weight, the other subcompartment, not the TBW total.
