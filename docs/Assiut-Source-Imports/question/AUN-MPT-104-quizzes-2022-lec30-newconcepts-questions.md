Labetalol antagonises both alpha and beta adrenoceptors

## question
A 34-year-old man is prescribed labetalol for hypertension. The effect on the cardiovascular system is a result of its action as an antagonist at which of the following?

## subject
fnd

## status

## answer_a
alpha-Adrenoceptors

## explanation_a
Incorrect as the sole answer. Labetalol does antagonise alpha1-adrenoceptors, but this is only part of its action.

Beta Adrenoceptors

## explanation_b
Incorrect as the sole answer. Labetalol does antagonise beta-adrenoceptors, but not exclusively -- it also blocks alpha1 receptors.
