## question
Which ONE of the following drugs is used to inhibit herpes viruses?

## subject

## status

## owner

C

## answer_a

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Azidothymidine (zidovudine) is an anti-HIV nucleoside reverse-transcriptase inhibitor, not a herpesvirus drug.

## answer_b
Oseltamivir

## explanation_b
Incorrect. Oseltamivir is a neuraminidase inhibitor used against influenza, not herpesviruses.

Acyclovir

## question
A 27-year-old nursing mother is diagnosed as suffering from genital herpes. She has a history of this viral infection and previously responded to a drug used topically. Apart from her current problem she is in good health. Which drug to be used orally is most likely to be prescribed at this time?

Incorrect. Ritonavir is an anti-HIV protease inhibitor, not a herpes simplex drug.

Oseltamivir can eradicate H1N1 influenza virus through:

## subject
pharm

## explanation_a

## explanation_b
Incorrect. Influenza is an RNA virus and oseltamivir does not target DNA replication.
