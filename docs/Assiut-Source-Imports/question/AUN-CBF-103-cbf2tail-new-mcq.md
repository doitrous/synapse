## title
Lipogenesis elongates the fatty acyl chain two carbons at a time

## question
An 18-year-old obese woman on a high-fat, high-carbohydrate, sedentary lifestyle has developed lipogenesis and obesity. Which statement correctly describes an aspect of lipogenesis (de novo fatty acid synthesis)?

## subject
fnd

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
D

## answer_a
Fatty acid synthesis and esterification to glycerol occur primarily in muscle cells

## explanation_a
Incorrect. De novo fatty acid synthesis occurs mainly in the liver and adipose tissue, not primarily in muscle cells.

## answer_b
The primary source of carbons for fatty acid synthesis is glycerol

## explanation_b
Incorrect. The carbon source for fatty acid synthesis is acetyl-CoA (delivered from the mitochondrion as citrate), not glycerol; glycerol supplies the backbone for esterification, not the acyl carbons.

## answer_c
NADP+ important for fatty acid synthesis is produced by the pentose phosphate pathway

## explanation_c
Incorrect. The pentose phosphate pathway produces NADPH (the reduced form), not NADP+; it is NADPH that supplies the reducing power for synthesis.

## answer_d
The fatty acyl chain on the fatty acid synthase complex is elongated two carbons at a time

## explanation_d
Correct. On the fatty acid synthase complex the growing fatty acyl chain is lengthened by two carbons in each cycle, using malonyl-CoA as the two-carbon donor and losing one carbon as CO2 per addition. This continues until palmitate (16 carbons) is released. Two-carbon growth is a defining feature of the synthase mechanism.

## answer_e
Fatty acids are synthesized from acetyl-CoA in the mitochondria

## explanation_e
Incorrect. Fatty acid synthesis is cytosolic; the mitochondrion is where fatty acids are oxidised, and the acetyl-CoA that starts synthesis is first exported to the cytosol as citrate.

## title
NADPH for fatty acid synthesis comes from glucose 6-phosphate dehydrogenase

## question
NADPH for fatty acid synthesis is produced mainly by the action of which of the following enzymes?

## subject
fnd

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
E

## answer_a
Acetyl-CoA carboxylase

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Acetyl-CoA carboxylase makes malonyl-CoA (the committed step of synthesis); it does not generate NADPH.

## answer_b
Pyruvate dehydrogenase

## explanation_b
Incorrect. Pyruvate dehydrogenase produces acetyl-CoA and NADH, not the NADPH used for fatty acid synthesis.

## answer_c
Glycerol kinase

## explanation_c
Incorrect. Glycerol kinase phosphorylates glycerol for triacylglycerol assembly; it generates no NADPH.

## answer_d
Lipoprotein lipase

## explanation_d
Incorrect. Lipoprotein lipase hydrolyses circulating triacylglycerol at the capillary wall; it is unrelated to NADPH production.

## answer_e
Glucose 6-phosphate dehydrogenase

## explanation_e
Correct. The NADPH spent in each reductive cycle of fatty acid synthesis is supplied mainly by the pentose phosphate (hexose monophosphate) pathway, whose first and rate-limiting enzyme is glucose 6-phosphate dehydrogenase. The malic enzyme reaction contributes a smaller share. This is why highly lipogenic tissues have an active pentose phosphate pathway.

## title
De novo fatty acid synthesis occurs in the cytosol

## question
In what compartment does de novo fatty acid synthesis occur?

## subject
fnd

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
B

## answer_a
Mitochondria

## explanation_a
Incorrect. The mitochondrion is the site of fatty acid beta-oxidation; the acetyl-CoA for synthesis is exported from it as citrate.

## answer_b
Cytosol

## explanation_b
Correct. De novo fatty acid synthesis takes place in the cytosol. Mitochondrial acetyl-CoA is carried out to the cytosol as citrate, released by ATP-citrate lyase, and assembled into a fatty acid on the cytosolic fatty acid synthase complex. The cytosolic location, using NADPH, contrasts with mitochondrial oxidation using NAD+ and FAD.

## answer_c
Peroxisome

## explanation_c
Incorrect. Peroxisomes carry out a specialised beta-oxidation of very-long-chain fatty acids, not de novo synthesis.

## answer_d
Endoplasmic reticulum

## explanation_d
Incorrect. The endoplasmic reticulum handles fatty acid elongation beyond C16 and desaturation, not the primary de novo synthesis of palmitate.

## title
The skin is innervated exclusively by the sympathetic division

## question
Which of the following organs gets its autonomic innervation exclusively from the sympathetic division?

## subject
neuro

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
D

## answer_a
Heart

## explanation_a
Incorrect. The heart receives a dual supply: sympathetic (accelerator) and parasympathetic (vagal, decelerator) fibres.

## answer_b
Penis

## explanation_b
Incorrect. The penis has both supplies -- parasympathetic (erection) and sympathetic (ejaculation).

## answer_c
Eyes

## explanation_c
Incorrect. The eye has both: sympathetic (pupillary dilatation) and parasympathetic (pupillary constriction, accommodation).

## answer_d
Skin

## explanation_d
Correct. The skin and its appendages -- sweat glands, arrector pili muscles and the great majority of cutaneous blood vessels -- receive a sympathetic supply only, with no parasympathetic input. This is one of the small set of structures that escape the usual dual autonomic pattern. The adrenal medulla is another sympathetic-only structure.

## answer_e
Lungs

## explanation_e
Incorrect. The lungs/bronchi receive both sympathetic (bronchodilator) and parasympathetic (vagal, bronchoconstrictor) fibres.
