## label
The zona pellucida keeps the cleaving zygote from enlarging as it divides

## canonical_key
zonapellucida.function.confines-cleaving-zygote-size

## definition
As the zygote cleaves, each mitotic division produces more, smaller blastomeres, but the intact zona pellucida is an unyielding acellular coat that confines the whole conceptus within a fixed outer size. The overall diameter of the dividing cell mass therefore stays essentially unchanged from the zygote through the morula stage, even as the number of cells inside multiplies -- growth is achieved by dividing, not by expanding.

## explicit_objective
State that the zona pellucida prevents the cleaving zygote from increasing in overall size as it divides.

bilaminar-disc.formation.epiblast-hypoblast-from-icm-week2

## definition
During the second week after fertilisation, the inner cell mass reorganises into two adjacent layers, forming the bilaminar embryonic disc. The layer facing the amniotic cavity is the epiblast (columnar cells), and the layer facing the blastocoele/yolk sac is the hypoblast (cuboidal cells) -- these are the first two germ layers to differentiate, preceding the three intraembryonic (ectoderm, mesoderm, endoderm) germ layers that gastrulation produces in the third week.

The extraembryonic coelom forms within, and is lined by, extraembryonic mesoderm

## canonical_key
extraembryonic-coelom.formation.within-lined-by-extraembryonic-mesoderm

## definition
Once extraembryonic mesoderm has formed, isolated spaces (lacunae) appear and coalesce within it, forming a single large cavity called the extraembryonic coelom (chorionic cavity). This cavity is entirely lined by extraembryonic mesoderm -- its somatic layer against the trophoblast/amnion, and its splanchnic layer against the yolk sac -- and its expansion eventually reduces the connecting stalk to the narrow body stalk that will become the umbilical cord.

## label
The zona pellucida surrounds the oocyte, zygote and cleaving stages through the early blastocyst, then disappears (hatching) before implantation

## canonical_key
zona-pellucida.persistence.surrounds-through-early-blastocyst-then-hatches

## definition
The zona pellucida is present from the primary oocyte stage onward, and continues to surround the zygote and every cleavage stage -- morula and early blastocyst alike -- keeping the conceptus at a constant overall size and preventing premature adhesion to the tubal wall. It does not persist all the way to implantation: the blastocyst must first "hatch," shedding the zona pellucida, before its trophoblast can make direct contact with, and attach to, the endometrium. The zona is therefore gone before implantation begins, not present until after it.
