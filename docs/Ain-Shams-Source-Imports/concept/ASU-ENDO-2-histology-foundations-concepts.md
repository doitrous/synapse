## label
Thyroid follicular epithelium changes with activity state

## id
CON-END-AC6999E3ABBC23

## canonical_key
thyroid.follicle.activity-morphology

## definition
In the ASU Year 2 endocrine histology source, thyroid follicular cells are cuboidal in a normal functioning follicle; in hyperactive follicles the cells become columnar and the follicle is smaller with little colloid, while in hypoactive follicles the cells become flat squamous and the follicle is distended with a large amount of colloid.

## explicit_objective
Compare normal, hyperactive and hypoactive thyroid follicles by follicular-cell shape, follicle size and colloid amount as taught in the ASU Zahra histology source.

## label
Follicular cells synthesize, store and release thyroid hormones through thyroglobulin

## id
CON-END-A11F28FA99B94D

## canonical_key
thyroid.follicular-cell.thyroglobulin-hormone-cycle

## definition
In the ASU Year 2 endocrine histology source, thyroid follicular cells form glycoprotein in rER and Golgi apparatus, discharge non-iodinated thyroglobulin into the follicular lumen, take up iodide, oxidize it to iodine, bind iodine to glycoprotein as iodinated thyroglobulin, and under TSH take thyroglobulin back up so lysosomal enzymes release T3 and T4.

## explicit_objective
Outline the ASU Zahra source's follicular-cell pathway for synthesis, storage and release of thyroid hormone through thyroglobulin.

...

 Under the effect of TSH, the follicular cells pick up thyroglobulin by
endocytosis forming pinocytotic vesicles inside cytoplasm.
 Lysozymes fuse with the pinocytotic vesicles then lysozomal enzymes
will break down thyroglobulin releasing tri and tetraiodothyronin (T3
and T4)
