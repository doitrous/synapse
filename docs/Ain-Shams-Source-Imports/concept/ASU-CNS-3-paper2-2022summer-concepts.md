## label
Dexamethasone, given with or just before the first dose of antibiotics, reduces neurological complications of bacterial meningitis

## id
CON-NEU-F499237CF4C1DB

## canonical_key
bacterialmeningitis.treatment.dexamethasone-with-first-antibiotic-dose

## definition
Adjunctive dexamethasone, given intravenously with or just before the first dose of antibiotic therapy for suspected or confirmed bacterial meningitis, reduces the inflammatory response generated as bacterial cell walls lyse under antibiotic action, thereby lowering the risk of neurological complications such as sensorineural hearing loss. Its benefit depends on early, first-dose timing — dexamethasone started after antibiotics have already been running for some time no longer meaningfully blunts this early lytic inflammatory surge, so it must be given immediately, not as an afterthought once treatment is under way.

## explicit_objective
State that dexamethasone is given with or immediately before the first dose of antibiotics in bacterial meningitis, to reduce neurological complications such as hearing loss.

## id
CON-NEU-CE603C6A3BCEC3

## canonical_key
parkinsondisease.pharmacology.quetiapine-for-psychosis

## definition
Psychosis (hallucinations, delusions) can complicate Parkinson disease, both from the disease itself and as an adverse effect of dopaminergic therapy, but most antipsychotics are potent D2 receptor antagonists that would worsen the patient's parkinsonism. Quetiapine, an atypical (second-generation) antipsychotic with comparatively weak and rapidly-dissociating D2 receptor blockade, is therefore preferred in this setting because it controls psychotic symptoms with a substantially lower risk of exacerbating extrapyramidal motor symptoms than typical antipsychotics or most other atypical agents.

## label
Habituation is a form of non-associative learning in which a response to a repeated, non-threatening stimulus progressively decreases

## id
CON-NEU-0DD9DF992AFAA3

## canonical_key
habituation.physiology.non-associative-learning

## definition
Habituation is a simple, non-associative form of learning in which the strength of a behavioural response to a repeated, unchanging stimulus progressively decreases as the nervous system learns that the stimulus is neither novel nor threatening and can safely be ignored. It requires no association between two stimuli or between a stimulus and a reinforcer (distinguishing it from classical and operant conditioning), and its opposite, sensitisation, is the enhancement of a response after exposure to a strong or noxious stimulus.

## label
The posterior communicating artery connects the internal carotid artery with the posterior cerebral artery, completing the circle of Willis

## id
CON-NEU-97589D82B069C5

## canonical_key
circleofwillis.posterior-communicating-artery.ica-pca-connection

## definition
The posterior communicating artery is a short anastomotic vessel that runs backward from the internal carotid artery to join the posterior cerebral artery on each side, forming part of the posterior half of the circle of Willis and linking the anterior (carotid) and posterior (vertebrobasilar) cerebral circulations. This anastomosis allows collateral blood flow between the carotid and vertebrobasilar systems if one is compromised, and its origin from the internal carotid artery is also one of the classic sites at which a congenital berry aneurysm forms, which can compress the adjacent oculomotor nerve and cause a painful third nerve palsy with pupillary involvement.

## id

migraine.pharmacology.sumatriptan-acute-treatment

## definition
Sumatriptan, a selective serotonin 5-HT1B/1D receptor agonist ("triptan"), is used for the acute (abortive) treatment of moderate-to-severe migraine attacks once a headache has already started, working by causing cranial vasoconstriction and inhibiting the release of vasoactive neuropeptides from trigeminal nerve terminals.
