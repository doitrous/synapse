CON-MSK-ASU-AE-LANGERS-LINES

## label
Langer's (cleavage) lines of the skin follow the direction of the collagen fibre bundles in the dermis

## canonical_key
skin.langers-lines.collagen-fibre-direction

## definition
Langer's lines (cleavage lines) are the natural lines of tension mapped over the surface of the skin. They run in the direction of the collagen fibre bundles of the reticular dermis, not the elastic fibres, the skin creases over joints or the ridge pattern of fingerprints -- all of which are separate surface markings with their own causes. A surgical incision placed parallel to Langer's lines cuts across fewer collagen bundles, so it gaps less, heals with less tension and leaves a finer scar than one placed across them.

## explicit_objective
State that Langer's lines represent the direction of the dermal collagen fibres, and give their surgical significance.

## id
CON-MSK-ASU-AE-TOE-ADDUCTION-AXIS

## label
Toe abduction and adduction are measured from the second toe, the long axis of the foot -- not the middle toe, as in the hand

## canonical_key
lower-limb.toe-movements.adduction-abduction-reference-axis-second-toe

## definition
In the upper limb, finger abduction and adduction are described relative to the middle (third) finger, the long axis of the hand. The lower limb uses a different axis: toe abduction and adduction are described relative to the second toe, the long axis of the foot. Adduction of a toe is movement towards the second toe; abduction is movement away from it. This is a standing point of confusion because students default to the hand's own middle-digit axis.

## explicit_objective
State that the reference axis for toe abduction/adduction is the second toe, not the middle toe, and contrast it with the hand's middle-finger axis.

CON-MSK-ASU-AE-CARPAL-BONE-COUNT

## label
The wrist has eight carpal bones in two rows of four, not seven

## canonical_key
hand.carpal-bones.eight-bones-two-rows-of-four

## definition
The carpus (wrist) is built of eight carpal bones arranged in two rows of four. The proximal row, from lateral to medial, is the scaphoid, lunate, triquetrum and pisiform (the last a sesamoid bone within the tendon of flexor carpi ulnaris); the distal row is the trapezium, trapezoid, capitate and hamate. A hand skeleton listed with only seven carpal bones is short one -- a common distractor against the correct count of eight.

## explicit_objective
State that the carpus has eight bones in two rows of four, and name them.
