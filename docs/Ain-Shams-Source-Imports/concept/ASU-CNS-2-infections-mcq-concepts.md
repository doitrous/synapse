## label
Progressive dementia in advanced HIV is most directly a result of HIV-1 meningoencephalitis

## id
CON-NEU-8595BF9865F503

## canonical_key
hivdementia.etiology.hiv1-meningoencephalitis

## definition
In advanced HIV infection, HIV-associated dementia (HIV encephalopathy) results directly from chronic HIV-1 infection of the central nervous system itself — HIV-1 meningoencephalitis with microglial nodules, multinucleated giant cells and white-matter pallor — rather than from an opportunistic co-infection. This distinguishes it from focal mass lesions such as toxoplasmosis or primary CNS lymphoma, or from progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy caused by JC virus, all of which are separate HIV-associated CNS complications with distinct pathology.

## label
Both tuberculous and fungal meningitis characteristically show a lymphocytic predominance and decreased CSF glucose

## id

## canonical_key
chronicmeningitis.csf.tb-and-fungal-shared-pattern

## definition
Tuberculous and fungal (e.g. cryptococcal) meningitis, despite differing causative organisms, share a similar chronic-meningitis CSF pattern: a lymphocyte-predominant pleocytosis with decreased glucose and elevated protein, reflecting the chronic granulomatous or fungal inflammatory response, which is why the two are grouped together and distinguished from each other by specific stains, cultures and antigen tests rather than by the basic CSF profile alone.

## explicit_objective
