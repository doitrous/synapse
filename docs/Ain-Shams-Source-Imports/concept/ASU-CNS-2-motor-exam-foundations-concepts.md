## id

## definition
In the ASU Year 2 CNS clinical medicine source, the UMN-versus-LMN table contrasts tone, deep tendon reflexes, plantar response, fasciculation and wasting: UMNL is listed with increased tone, increased deep tendon reflexes, extensor plantar response, absent fasciculation and late wasting, while LMNL is listed with reduced tone, reduced or absent deep tendon reflexes, normal or absent plantar response, possible fasciculation and usually present wasting.
