Jobe test assesses supraspinatus by resisted empty-can positioning

## definition
In the ASU Year 1 Locomotor clinical-medicine practical, Jobe test is a shoulder special test for supraspinatus in which both arms are placed in 90 degrees abduction and 30 degrees flexion with thumbs down, then resisted downward pressure is applied.
