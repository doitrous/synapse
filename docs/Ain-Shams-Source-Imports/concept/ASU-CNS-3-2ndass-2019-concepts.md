## label
The stria medullaris thalami connects the septal area to the habenular nucleus

## id
CON-NEU-5E65180FDA9928

## canonical_key
striamedullaristhalami.connection.septal-area-to-habenular-nucleus

## definition
The stria medullaris thalami is a slender fibre bundle running along the dorsomedial margin of the thalamus that carries afferents from the septal area (and other limbic forebrain structures) to the habenular nucleus, forming one of the principal inputs of the habenulo-limbic system. From the habenular nucleus, the habenulointerpeduncular tract (fasciculus retroflexus) continues on to the midbrain, linking the limbic forebrain to brainstem monoaminergic centres.

## label
Cocaine is unique among local anaesthetics for producing intrinsic vasoconstriction

## id
CON-NEU-6A1691FE298E84

## canonical_key
cocaine.property.intrinsic-vasoconstriction-local-anesthetic

## definition
Cocaine is a local anaesthetic that blocks voltage-gated sodium channels like other agents in its class, but is unique among clinically used local anaesthetics in also blocking presynaptic reuptake of noradrenaline, producing local vasoconstriction as an intrinsic property of the drug itself. Other local anaesthetics (e.g. lidocaine, bupivacaine) instead tend to cause vasodilation and are typically combined with an added vasoconstrictor (e.g. adrenaline) to prolong their action and limit systemic absorption.

## explicit_objective
State that cocaine, unlike other local anaesthetics, produces intrinsic vasoconstriction rather than requiring an added vasoconstrictor.

## id
CON-NEU-7C4D3396BF2C01

## canonical_key
workingmemory.function.holds-recently-read-words-during-sentence-processing

## definition
Working memory is the short-term, limited-capacity system that actively holds and manipulates information for ongoing cognitive tasks. When reading a sentence, the first words must be held online in working memory so they can be integrated with the words that follow to construct the sentence's meaning — a classic illustration of working memory's role in language comprehension, distinct from long-term memory systems (episodic, semantic) that store information over much longer timescales, and from the brief, unprocessed sensory memory buffer.

## label
Stimulation of the posterior (lateral) hypothalamus produces tachycardia via sympathetic activation

## id
CON-NEU-D43CD404FF91C7

## canonical_key
posteriorhypothalamus.stimulation.sympathetic-tachycardia

## definition
The posterior (lateral) hypothalamus is the principal hypothalamic centre for sympathetic outflow. Its stimulation produces a coordinated sympathetic response — tachycardia, vasoconstriction and shivering (heat-conserving/heat-generating responses) — but not sweating, which is a cholinergic sympathetic response mediated by a different hypothalamic region. This contrasts with the anterior hypothalamus, which is chiefly associated with parasympathetic outflow and heat-dissipating responses.

## explicit_objective
