## definition
The active site is a special pocket or cleft formed by protein folding. It contains amino acid side chains that participate in substrate binding and catalysis, so the substrate can form an enzyme-substrate complex and proceed through the induced-fit catalytic step.
## explicit_objective
Describe the active site and outline how substrate binding leads to catalysis in the Ain Shams lecture 1 sequence.
## pitfalls
Treating the active site as only a binding groove and omitting the catalytic amino acid side chains.
