## explanation_a
Incorrect. Fertilisation is when the SECOND meiotic division completes, not the first -- this option swaps the two meiotic divisions' timing.

## answer_b
Gastrulation

## explanation_b
Incorrect. Gastrulation is a week-3 embryonic event, long after both meiotic divisions of the oocyte have been triggered.

## answer_c
Implantation

## explanation_c
Incorrect. Implantation occurs after fertilisation, well after the first meiotic division has already completed.

## answer_d
Puberty

## explanation_d
Incorrect. Puberty is when the prophase-I arrest ENDS and resumption becomes possible, not when the division itself completes for a given oocyte.
