## title
Number of somites on the 22nd day

## question
The number of somites during the 22nd day is:

## subject
dev

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
C

## answer_a
Two

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Two somites would fall well before the first day of segmentation even reaches its first full pair -- the first occipital somite pair appears on day 20, so two somites does not match any day in the regular sequence tested here.

## answer_b
Four

## explanation_b
Incorrect. Four somites is the correct count for day 21 (the first pair on day 20, plus three more added on day 21), one day earlier than the question asks about.

## answer_c
Seven

## explanation_c
Correct. The first pair of occipital somites separates on day 20, and from day 21 onward three pairs are added every day during the regular "somite period" (day 21 to day 30). So day 21 gives 1+3=4, and day 22 gives 4+3=7 -- the printed answer key's own worked arithmetic for this exact row. The same formula (somites = 1 + 3x(day-20)) is what the companion question on the day-20 onset (embryo3-q034) and the day-23/day-26 duplicates of this row both rest on.

## answer_d
Ten

## explanation_d
Incorrect. Ten somites is the count for day 23, one day after the day this question asks about -- the classic 'off by one day' distractor this timetable is built to test.

## answer_e
Thirteen

## explanation_e
Incorrect. Thirteen somites would need two more days of segmentation past day 22 (roughly day 24), well past the day this question specifies.

# Item

## id
QST-DEV-ASU-AE-EMBRYO3-FIRST-SOMITE-DAY

## title
Day the earliest somites appear

## question
The earliest somites begin to appear on day:

## subject
dev

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
C

## answer_a
15

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Day 15 is still within the second week's own timetable (implantation and the bilaminar disc), well before the third-week structures -- primitive streak, notochord, and then the first somites -- have even begun to form.

## answer_b
18

## explanation_b
Incorrect. Day 18 falls within the third week, after gastrulation has started, but the first somite pair has not yet separated from the paraxial mesoderm at this point -- segmentation begins two days later.

## answer_c
20

## explanation_c
Correct. The first pair of occipital somites separates from the paraxial mesoderm on day 20, marking the start of the regular "somite period" during which three pairs are added each day through day 30. This day-20 onset is the anchor date the whole somite-count-by-day formula (tested from the other direction at embryo3-q017) is built on.

## answer_d
25

## explanation_d
Incorrect. Day 25 is well into the somite period by this point (roughly 20-21 pairs already present by the formula) -- it is not when somites first begin to appear, but a day on which many are already present.

## answer_e
28

## explanation_e
Incorrect. Day 28 is even later in the somite period, close to where the regular three-pairs-a-day rate starts to slow toward the irregular late phase -- far past the true onset day.
