## explanation_a
Incorrect. IL-4 is a signature Th2, not Th1, cytokine.

IL-5

## explanation_b
Incorrect. IL-5 is a signature Th2 cytokine, promoting eosinophil activity.

IL-7

## explanation_c

## answer_a
CRP

## explanation_a
Incorrect. CRP is an acute-phase protein that enhances phagocytosis and complement activation, not the early antiviral mediator described here.

## answer_b

## explanation_b
Incorrect. Mannose-binding lectin is a complement-pathway acute-phase protein, not the early antiviral cytokine described here.

## answer_c
Interferon-alpha

## explanation_c
Correct. Type I interferon (interferon-alpha) is produced early in viral infection, chiefly by the infected cell itself and by plasmacytoid dendritic cells, and acts on neighbouring, still-uninfected cells to induce an intracellular antiviral state that slows the infection's spread to adjoining cells. ## explanation_d
Incorrect. IgM is the first antibody isotype in a primary humoral response, but it is not the rapid, non-antibody antiviral mediator this question describes.
