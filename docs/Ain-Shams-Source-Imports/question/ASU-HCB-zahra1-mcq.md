## title
Organelles characterised by self-duplication

## question
Which of the following organelles is characterised by self-duplication?

## subject
fnd

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
D

## answer_a
Centrioles.

## explanation_a
Incorrect alone. Centrioles genuinely do self-duplicate once per cell cycle, but they are not the only self-duplicating organelle among the options.

## answer_b
Golgi complex.

## explanation_b
Incorrect. The Golgi complex has no self-duplication mechanism; it is continuously rebuilt from membrane and protein delivered by the secretory pathway rather than duplicating itself.

## answer_c
Mitochondria.

## explanation_c
Incorrect alone. Mitochondria genuinely divide by fission to increase their own numbers, but they are not the only self-duplicating organelle among the options.

## answer_d
Both A and C are correct.

## explanation_d
Correct. Centrioles and mitochondria are both self-replicating organelles: a centriole nucleates the growth of a new, perpendicular daughter centriole once each cell cycle, and a mitochondrion divides by fission, both without needing to be built anew from unrelated organelles. The Golgi complex, by contrast, has no equivalent autonomous duplication step; it is instead continuously supplied and rebuilt from membrane and protein arriving via the rough endoplasmic reticulum and the endocytic pathway. Recognising that self-duplication is a property shared specifically by centrioles and mitochondria, and not by the Golgi, is exactly what this question tests.

Which of the following share in the maintenance and renewal of the cell membrane?

## subject

## answer_b

## explanation_b
Incorrect. Lysosomes play no part in membrane maintenance or renewal; their role on fusing with other vesicles is digestive, not membrane-supplying.

## answer_c

Recognising the Golgi and coated vesicles, and not lysosomes, as the two routes of membrane maintenance and renewal is exactly what this question tests.

## subject

Incorrect. Only the first statement (subunit A = 13 protofilaments) is true; the other two misattribute the protofilament counts, so not all the previous statements can be correct together.
