## summary
This Ain Shams Year 3 communication-skills article covers the lecture definition of active listening, the definition of reflective listening, and the listed listening skills of eye contact, distance, gesture, environment and interested silence.
## sections
### Definition
Active listening involves hearing and understanding what another person communicates through words and body language, then responding by reflecting the thoughts and feelings heard.

### Mechanism
Reflective listening means hearing and understanding, then letting the other person know that they are being heard and understood.

### Key determinants
The listening skills listed in the lecture are eye contact, distance, gesture, environment, and interested silence.

### Clinical significance
In this lecture, active listening is taught as useful for both speaker and listener and as part of handling resistance or anger; this article does not add recommendations beyond the local lecture.
## body

## hold_these
Active listening is more than hearing; it includes a reflective response.
Reflective listening lets the other person know that they are being heard and understood.
The listed listening skills are eye contact, distance, gesture, environment and interested silence.
## lose_the_mark
Describing active listening as passive silence only.
Listing communication behaviours that are not in the lecture as if they were the taught listening-skills set.

## summary
This Ain Shams Year 3 communication-skills article narrows to reflecting skills: the checking-out process that expresses content and feeling and verifies small segments of another person’s communication.
## sections
### Definition
Reflecting skills are a checking-out process that expresses the essence of content and feeling and verifies small segments of communication.

### Mechanism
The listener responds by expressing the essence of what the other person has communicated, including content and feeling, then checks the accuracy of that perception.

### Key determinants
The lecture frames reflecting skills as small-segment checking rather than a broad judgement of the whole conversation.

### Clinical significance
The lecture places reflecting skills inside active listening; this article does not add clinical standards beyond that local curriculum statement.
## body

## hold_these
Reflecting skills are a checking-out process.
The reflected material includes both content and feeling.
Checking happens in small segments of communication.
## lose_the_mark
Reflecting content while ignoring feeling.
Treating reflecting as a broad summary of the entire conversation rather than checking small segments.
