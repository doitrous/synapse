## summary
This ASU Year 1 Clinical Skills slice separates the brief presenting complaint from the structured history of present illness. It is based on a local lecture and remains evidence-gated pending independent clinical-skills review.

## sections
### Purpose and indications
Use this lecture-grounded sequence to record the presenting complaint and then organise the history of present illness. It is a curriculum summary, not a complete clinical standard.

### Preparation and consent
The selected source pages do not provide a preparation or consent checklist for these two history elements. No additional steps are supplied from outside the authorised source.

### Equipment
The selected source pages do not specify equipment.

### Step-by-step procedure
Record the presenting complaint in the patient's own words and include its duration.

The lecture then places symptoms in chronological order and analyses site, onset, course, character, radiation, associations, timing, aggravating factors and severity.

It also names open, closed and leading questions, medical terminology of symptoms, and review of symptoms from other systems.

### Safety and complications
This local lecture is not independent clinical verification. Do not treat this short source slice as a complete safety, privacy or consent standard.

### Communication points
Keep the presenting complaint in the patient's own words. The lecture distinguishes question types when moving into the detailed history of present illness.

### Assessment criteria
The learner should identify the patient's own words and duration as the presenting complaint, then reproduce the chronological and symptom-analysis structure stated in the lecture.

## hold_these
The presenting complaint uses the patient's own words and includes duration.
The detailed current history is organised chronologically and analysed by the listed symptom dimensions.

## lose_the_mark
Replacing the patient's words with a diagnosis in the presenting complaint.
Listing symptoms without chronology or structured analysis.

## summary
This ASU Year 1 Clinical Skills slice collects the past-history and social-history components explicitly listed in the local history-taking lecture. It remains evidence-gated pending independent clinical-skills review.

## sections
### Purpose and indications
Use this lecture-grounded checklist to distinguish the background elements placed under past history from the domains placed under social history.

### Preparation and consent
The selected source pages do not provide a preparation or consent checklist for these two history elements. No additional steps are supplied from outside the authorised source.

### Equipment
The selected source pages do not specify equipment.

### Step-by-step procedure
For past history, ask about similar conditions, other diseases, previous hospital admission, operations, accidents, blood transfusion and allergy.

For social history, the lecture lists home life, education, relationships, sexual history and substance misuse, with the stated aim of planning a proper decision.

### Safety and complications
This local lecture is not independent clinical verification. It does not supply a complete privacy, safeguarding or sensitive-question standard for taking a social history.

### Communication points
The lecture lists the domains to cover but does not prescribe exact question wording. No scripts are added from memory.

### Assessment criteria
The learner should assign the listed previous conditions and healthcare events to past history, and the listed personal and social domains to social history.

## hold_these
Past history includes previous conditions, admissions, procedures, accidents, transfusion and allergy in this lecture.
Social history includes home life, education, relationships, sexual history and substance misuse.

## lose_the_mark
Placing previous surgery outside past history.
Reducing social history to substance misuse alone.
