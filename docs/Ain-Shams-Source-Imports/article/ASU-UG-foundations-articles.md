## summary
This Ain Shams Year 3 renal-anatomy article covers the lecture statements on kidney vertebral level, the lower position of the right kidney, and the external feature set of poles, borders and surfaces.

## sections
### Definition
The kidneys extend from the upper border of T12 to L3, and the right kidney lies half an inch lower because of its relation to the liver.

### Mechanism
This local lecture presents the lower right kidney position as related to the liver; this article does not add a mechanism beyond that source statement.

### Key determinants
Each kidney has two poles, two borders and two surfaces.

### Clinical significance
This is a gross-anatomy orientation slice, not a treatment or procedural rule.

### Common misconceptions
A common error is to place both kidneys at the same level or to list only one category of external feature.

## body

## hold_these
The kidneys extend from the upper border of T12 to L3.
The right kidney is lower because of its relation to the liver.
Each kidney has two poles, two borders and two surfaces.

## lose_the_mark
Placing both kidneys at exactly the same level.
Forgetting one of the three external-feature categories: poles, borders or surfaces.

## summary
This Ain Shams Year 3 renal-anatomy article narrows to kidney pole orientation and renal hilum arrangement: the superomedial long axis places the superior pole nearer the median plane, and the hilum is arranged anterior renal vein, intermediate renal artery and posterior renal pelvis.

## sections
### Definition
The superior pole of the kidney is nearer the median plane than the inferior pole because the kidney long axis is directed superomedially.

### Mechanism
The lecture links this pole orientation to the direction of the kidney long axis and the relation of the superior pole to the suprarenal gland.

### Key determinants
In the renal hilum, the renal vein is anterior, the renal artery is intermediate and the renal pelvis is posterior.

### Clinical significance
This is a gross-anatomy orientation slice, not a treatment or procedural rule.

### Common misconceptions
A common error is to reverse the hilum arrangement by placing the renal pelvis anterior to the vessels.

## body

## hold_these
The superior pole is nearer the median plane than the inferior pole.
The kidney long axis is directed superomedially.
The renal hilum is arranged anterior renal vein, intermediate renal artery and posterior renal pelvis.

## lose_the_mark
Drawing the kidney long axis as vertical.
Putting the renal pelvis anterior to the renal vessels in the hilum.
