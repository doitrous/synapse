In this ASU motor examination source, the UMN-versus-LMN table contrasts tone, deep tendon reflexes, plantar response, fasciculation and wasting.

### Safety and complications

### Assessment criteria

## hold_these
UMNL is listed with increased tone, increased deep tendon reflexes, extensor plantar response, absent fasciculation and late wasting.
LMNL is listed with reduced tone, reduced or absent deep tendon reflexes, normal or absent plantar response, possible fasciculation and usually present wasting.
