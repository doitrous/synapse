## support_span
Functions of thyroid gland
  ❶ Synthesis, storage and Release of Thyroid Hormone (The main function)
through the following steps:
(1) Synthesis & storage of thyroglobulin:
 Glycoprotein is formed in rER and Golgi apparatus of the follicular cell.
 The glycoprotein (non-iodinated thyroglobulin) is discharged by
exocytosis into the follicular lumen.
 Follicular cells pick up iodide from the blood stream by active
transport mechanism (via the basolateral cell membrane).
 Iodide is pumped to the lumen to be oxidized to iodine by membrane-
bound thyroperoxidase enzyme on the microvilli surfaces of
thyrocytes then to the follicular lumen.
 Iodine then binds to the glycoproteins forming the iodinated
thyroglobulin (glycoprotein and iodine), which is stored in the lumen.
(2) Release of thyroid hormones
 Under the effect of TSH, the follicular cells pick up thyroglobulin by
endocytosis forming pinocytotic vesicles inside cytoplasm.
 Lysozymes fuse with the pinocytotic vesicles then lysozomal enzymes
will break down thyroglobulin releasing tri and tetraiodothyronin (T3
and T4), which will diffuse from the cells to reach the blood capillaries.
