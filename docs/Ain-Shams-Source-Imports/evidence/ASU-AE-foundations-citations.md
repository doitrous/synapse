Synovial joints
 Bones are held together by
fibrous capsule that is lined
by synovial membrane that
secretes synovial fluid.
 Articular ends are covered by
hyaline cartilage.
 The capsule is thickened in
certain parts to form
ligaments, which provide
strength and flexibility to the
joint.
 Synovial joints are freely
mobile and represent most
joints of the body.
