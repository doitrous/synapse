## text
The ASU Year 1 infection practical teaches that Sabouraud dextrose agar contains Sabouraud's dextrose broth at pH 5.6 plus agar, includes chloramphinicol, cyclohexidine and acidic pH as selective features, and is used for isolation of fungi.
