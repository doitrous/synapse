## subject
The three main RNA types

## predicate
differ by

## object
tRNA carrying the highest percentage of modified nucleotides, mRNA being the most sequence-heterogeneous species, a minimum of roughly 20 tRNA species existing (one per amino acid), and mRNA being the sole coding RNA among rRNA/tRNA/snRNA/lncRNA

## display_text
Among RNA types, tRNA has the highest percentage of modified nucleotides, mRNA is the most heterogeneous ("hnRNA") species, a human cell needs at least ~20 tRNA species (one per amino acid), and mRNA is the only coding RNA among rRNA, tRNA, snRNA and lncRNA.

## concept_id
CON-FND-297C617E70A8FA

## subject
RNA

## predicate
is chemically distinguished from DNA by

## object
its ribose 2'-OH group, which makes RNA susceptible to alkaline hydrolysis (alkali-labile) while DNA is alkali-stable, alongside RNA's generally shorter length, single-strandedness with intra-strand secondary structure, and use of uracil instead of thymine

## display_text
RNA is alkali-labile because its ribose 2'-OH group drives alkaline hydrolysis of the backbone, unlike DNA; RNA is also generally shorter, single-stranded (though it folds into intra-strand hydrogen-bonded secondary structure), and uses uracil in place of thymine.
