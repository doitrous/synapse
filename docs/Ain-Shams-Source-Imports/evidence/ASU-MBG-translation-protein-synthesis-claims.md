## subject
Prokaryotic translation initiation

## predicate
begins with

## object
30S-subunit binding to the Shine-Dalgarno sequence by hydrogen bonding to 16S rRNA, and loads formylmethionyl-tRNA (fMet-tRNAfMet) directly into the P site rather than via the A site

## display_text
Prokaryotic translation initiation begins when the 30S ribosomal subunit binds the mRNA's Shine-Dalgarno sequence by base pairing with 16S rRNA; the initiator tRNA, carrying N-formylmethionine, is loaded directly into the P site rather than entering via the A site the way elongator tRNAs do.

## predicate
includes

## object

## subject
Peptidyl transferase

## predicate

## object

Peptidyl transferase is a ribozyme: its peptide-bond-forming (and, at termination, hydrolytic) activity is carried by ribosomal RNA itself — 28S rRNA in the eukaryotic 60S subunit, 23S rRNA in the prokaryotic 50S subunit — not by a distinct protein enzyme.
