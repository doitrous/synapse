Bacterial RNA polymerase holoenzyme

## predicate
initiates transcription through

## object

## display_text
Bacterial RNA polymerase is a single multi-subunit holoenzyme (core enzyme plus sigma factor) that transcribes every RNA class; its sigma subunit recognises the Pribnow box, consensus TATAAT, to initiate transcription at the correct site.

# Item

## id
CLM-FND-RHO-INTRINSIC-TERMINATION-01

## concept_id
CON-FND-E5651C6097AEC7

## subject
Bacterial transcription termination

## predicate
occurs by

## object
one of two mechanisms — rho-dependent, needing the rho protein to catch the polymerase, or intrinsic (rho-independent), needing only a G-C-rich hairpin followed by a poly-U run in the new RNA — and neither initiation nor termination requires a dedicated helicase, since RNA polymerase unwinds the DNA it transcribes on its own

## display_text
Bacterial transcription terminates either through the rho protein catching up to RNA polymerase (rho-dependent) or through a G-C-rich hairpin plus a poly-U run in the transcript destabilising the RNA-DNA hybrid on its own (intrinsic/rho-independent); no dedicated helicase is needed for transcription the way one is for replication.
