## id
CLM-FND-PROTO-ONCOGENE-TSG-DEFINITIONS-01

## concept_id
CON-FND-1BCB86AE1C6B66

## subject
Proto-oncogenes and tumor suppressor genes

## predicate
are

## object
functionally opposite gene classes that both operate in every healthy cell: a proto-oncogene's product promotes division and a gain-of-function mutation activates it into an oncogene, while a tumor suppressor gene's product restrains division and a loss-of-function mutation inactivates it; RB, p53 and CDK inhibitors are tumor suppressor genes, Cerb-B2/c-Myc/Bcl-2 are oncogenes

## display_text
Proto-oncogenes and tumor suppressor genes are functionally opposite: a proto-oncogene's normal product promotes division and is activated into an oncogene by a gain-of-function mutation, while a tumor suppressor gene's normal product restrains division and is inactivated by a loss-of-function mutation — both function in every healthy cell, not only in cancer cells.
