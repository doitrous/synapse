## concept_id

## subject
Penetrance

## predicate
is defined as, and distinguished from expressivity by

## object
the proportion of individuals carrying a particular genotype who show any phenotype at all, whereas expressivity measures the severity of the phenotype among those who are affected

## display_text
Penetrance is the proportion of a genotype's carriers who show any phenotype at all; expressivity is how severe the phenotype is among those who do show it — two independent properties of the same genotype.

Translational-level regulation of gene expression

## predicate

## object
