## description
Local Ain Shams Year 1 Introduction to Anatomy and Embryology "MCQs - Embryo 3.pdf" answer bank (CamScanner scan, OCR'd, 20 pages), the third of a coherent three-part "Embryo 1/2/3" embryology series (see `coverage/ASU-AE-triage.md` blocks B/F/G/H for Embryo 1/2). The stem pages (1-16) OCR reasonably but scramble two-column short-answer layouts (chromosomal-formula and numeric questions in particular) and the two syndrome-matching blocks (Q55-59, Q60-64) badly enough that both were re-rendered at 600dpi and read by eye; the printed "Answers of MCQs" table (pp.17-20) OCR'd with the letter column intact throughout but the remark column truncated/misaligned on p.18-20 by the scan's curvature and a torn right edge, so every key row past 35 was re-rendered at 600dpi and cross-checked against each stem's own option wording. This caught two within-file reordered-duplicate questions (Q21, Q27, both re-askings of Q19's Klinefelter-genotype fact) and one apparent source key error (Q10: printed letter "e"/"Heart" and remark "Pia matter" both disagree with the standard-teaching answer, melanocyte; the printed letter is retained per house rule with the discrepancy logged in the question's own author_notes). Q1-55 were authored by lane 11 (2 held as duplicates, 53 question records); Q56-104 were authored by lane 12 (49 question records, 0 held), completing the paper at 102 authored questions total.
