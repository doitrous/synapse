## description
Native-text, 9-page ASU Year 3 CNS "EOM - CNS final paper 2 2024" final
examination (pages 1-6: 5-subject Anatomy/Physio/Pharma/Patho/Histo
embedded-answer MCQ block; pages 6-9: 6-question essay block, 15 discrete
facts). 29 new mints (10 anatomy/embryology, 8 physiology, 7 pharmacology,
3 pathology, 1 histology) plus one fact (cerebral infarction as liquefactive
necrosis) reused via overlay against an existing pending Kasr 108-INT
concept rather than re-minted. Micro, Para and Community items (Botulism,
meningitis pathogen, CMV/HSV encephalitis, rabies, Naegleria, Sparganum,
secondary prevention) are out of this lane's neuro subject scope, excluded
like biochemistry. Three items held as not medically defensible or
unrecoverable from the printed text: a "slow wave sleep has a low threshold
of arousal" item contradicts standard sleep physiology (deep NREM sleep is
classically the hardest stage to rouse someone from); a "hypotonia + left
occipital lobe + gliosis of caudate nucleus" item does not cohere into a
single recognisable diagnosis; a reading-comprehension item ("first two
words are read by working memory") could not be confidently supported from
standard physiology teaching. This catalogue row is rights-deferred and
does not approve learner-facing file access or deep links.
