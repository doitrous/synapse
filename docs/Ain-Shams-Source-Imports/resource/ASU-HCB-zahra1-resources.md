## description
Local Ain Shams Year 1 Histology and Cell Biology scanned cytology MCQ bank ("MCQ IN HISTOLOGY", Dr. Ahmed Zahra), a CamScanner-style scan with no PDF text layer, OCR'd across all 41 pages and cross-checked by rendering pages to images for the printed "Model answer" key tables at the end of each part. 322 questions across three parts (Part A 70Q, Part B 103Q, Part C 149Q), all cytology, covering plasma membrane, organelles, cytoskeleton, nucleus/chromatin, cytoplasmic inclusions and a handful of organelle-dysfunction clinical correlations. Heavy overlap with the module's already-authored blocks A (cell-mcq, epithelium-mcq) and B (cellhz1-mcq); this batch authors only the genuinely new/variant facts found on a full per-question dedup pass of Part A, Part B and a sample of Part C, recording every disposition in coverage/ASU-HCB-triage-keys.txt and the module ledger. Learner-facing concept approval, article inclusion and deep links are intentionally deferred until this bank is rights-cleared for student file use.
