## Why the two minters disagree, and what is still undecided

Two tools mint concept IDs and they hash different things:

```
scripts/kasr/seeds/types.ts    sha256("kau:" + module + ":" + key)
tools/mint-concept-id.mjs      sha256(key)          <- no module, no university
```

The unsalted one produced 237 of the IDs in the Kasr tree — all of 108 INT,
104 CPS practical, most of 103 BMS. It is module-blind and
university-blind, so two modules, or two universities, minting the same key
get the same ID. The salted one deliberately keeps them distinct.

These encode opposite answers to one product question: **are two modules
teaching the same idea one concept or two?** Until that is decided, §1 will
keep reappearing wherever the two conventions meet. It is not a tooling
defect to be patched — either answer is coherent, and the tools should be
made to agree with whichever is chosen.
