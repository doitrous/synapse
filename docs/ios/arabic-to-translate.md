the English should stay (a proper noun, or a term Egyptian students use in
English anyway) — a blank is a decision, not an omission, and nothing will
be re-asked about it.

**Who this is for.** Undergraduate medical students at Egyptian universities.
Where a clinical term is normally said in English in an Egyptian lecture
theatre, keeping it in English is the right answer.
