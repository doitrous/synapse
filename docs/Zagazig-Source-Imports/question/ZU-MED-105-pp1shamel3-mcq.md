## explanation_b
Incorrect. Forming is characterised by ambiguity and confusion about tasks, the earliest stage, well before the full goal-focus this stem describes.

## answer_c
Performing

## explanation_c
Correct. Performing is the stage of team dynamics this source marks as the team focusing all its attention on achieving goals.
