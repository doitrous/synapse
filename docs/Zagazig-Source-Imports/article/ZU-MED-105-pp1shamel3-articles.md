### Definition
Law is the system of rules a particular country or community recognizes as regulating the actions of its members, enforceable by the imposition of penalties, distinguished from culture, which this source defines as all the ways of life, including arts, beliefs and institutions, of a population passed down across generations. Bioethics helps answer three main questions together: which decisions are morally right or acceptable, how that decision is justified in moral terms, and the method or manner of the response when the decision is acted on. Principles that guide medical decision making include that autonomy is not merely a right but also a responsibility, that respect for patient autonomy by physicians is crucial since most patients feel dependent on physicians, and that patients have a right to refuse treatment but cannot claim all treatments. A competent adult's right to make decisions about what happens to their own body is a specific application of autonomy.
