## title
Reticulocyte features

## question
Concerning reticulocytes:

## subject
haem

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
B

## answer_a
They are smaller than mature erythrocytes

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Reticulocytes are about the same size as, or slightly larger than, mature erythrocytes, not smaller; they have not yet finished the membrane remodelling that shapes the final biconcave disc.

## answer_b
Appear in peripheral blood not more than 2%

## explanation_b
Correct. Reticulocytes normally make up no more than about 1-2% of circulating erythrocytes in peripheral blood. Brilliant cresyl blue, a supravital stain, is what demonstrates them, precipitating their residual ribosomal RNA into a visible reticular network — the feature the reticulocyte is named for and the reason it is counted as a marker of ongoing erythropoiesis.

## answer_c
Contain well-developed nucleus

## explanation_c
Incorrect. The reticulocyte, like the mature erythrocyte, has already extruded its nucleus at the preceding (normoblast) stage; it does not carry a well-developed nucleus.

## answer_d
Contains numerous rER

## explanation_d
Incorrect. The reticulocyte's residual ribonucleic material is scattered ribosomal RNA, not organised rough endoplasmic reticulum; that RNA is what the supravital stain precipitates into the visible reticulum.

## answer_e
The first precursor cell in erythropoiesis

## explanation_e
Incorrect. The proerythroblast, not the reticulocyte, is the first precursor cell of erythropoiesis; the reticulocyte is the last stage before the mature erythrocyte, at the opposite end of the series.

## title
What reticulocytes give rise to (past-exam block, دفعة 63)

## question
Reticulocytes give rise to:

## subject
haem

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
A

## answer_a
Erythrocytes

## explanation_a
Correct. The reticulocyte is the last stage of the erythroid series before the mature erythrocyte. Once it loses its residual ribosomal RNA and completes its membrane remodelling, it becomes the mature, biconcave, haemoglobin-filled erythrocyte.

## answer_b
Granulocytes

## explanation_b
Incorrect. Granulocytes arise from a separate myeloid series (granulopoiesis), not from the reticulocyte, which belongs to the erythroid series.

## answer_c
Platelets

## explanation_c
Incorrect. Platelets are fragments of the megakaryocyte, a separate series (thrombopoiesis) from the erythroid series the reticulocyte belongs to.

## answer_d
Agranulocytes

## explanation_d
Incorrect. "Agranulocytes" (lymphocytes and monocytes) are not a maturation product of the reticulocyte; they arise from entirely separate lymphoid and monocytic lines.

## answer_e
Lymphocytes

## explanation_e
Incorrect. Lymphocytes arise from the lymphoid stem cell line, not from the erythroid series the reticulocyte is the terminal step of.

## title
Reticulocyte cytoplasm (past-exam block, دفعة 63)

## question
Reticulocyte:

## subject
haem

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
D

## answer_a
Represent 10% of erythrocytes in peripheral blood

## explanation_a
Incorrect. Reticulocytes normally represent no more than about 1-2% of circulating erythrocytes, not 10%; a figure that high would signal markedly accelerated erythropoiesis.

## answer_b
Has high hemoglobin

## explanation_b
Incorrect. The reticulocyte has not yet accumulated its full haemoglobin content; haemoglobin synthesis continues into this stage rather than already being complete, so its haemoglobin content is lower than the mature erythrocyte's, not higher.

## answer_c
Highly dividing cell

## explanation_c
Incorrect. The reticulocyte, like the mature erythrocyte, has already lost the capacity to divide; mitotic capacity is lost earlier in the series, at the polychromatophilic erythroblast stage.

## answer_d
Basophilic cytoplasm

## explanation_d
Correct. The reticulocyte's cytoplasm stains basophilic with a supravital stain. That basophilia comes from residual ribosomal RNA left over from the preceding stage. Brilliant cresyl blue precipitates that RNA into the visible reticular network the cell is named for.

## answer_e
Has a nucleus

## explanation_e
Incorrect. The reticulocyte has no nucleus; nuclear extrusion happens at the normoblast stage that precedes it.
