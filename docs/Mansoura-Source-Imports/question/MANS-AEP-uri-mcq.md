## title
Location of the trigone

## question
The trigone is located in ……...

## subject
renal

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
B

## answer_a
Apex of urinary bladder.

## explanation_a
Incorrect. The apex of the bladder is its anterosuperior point, opposite the base where the trigone actually lies.

## answer_b
Base of urinary bladder.

## explanation_b
Correct. The trigone is a smooth triangular area on the inner aspect of the bladder base (its posteroinferior wall), in contrast to the folded (rugose) mucosa lining the rest of the bladder. Its corners are marked by the two ureteric orifices above and the internal urethral orifice below. This base location is the trigone's defining landmark.

## answer_c
Ureter.

## explanation_c
Incorrect. The ureter is a separate tube proximal to the bladder, not the site of the trigone itself.

## answer_d
Urethra.

## explanation_d
Incorrect. The urethra begins at the trigone's lower corner (the internal urethral orifice) but is not itself the trigone.

## answer_e
Tip of penis

## explanation_e
Incorrect. The tip of the penis is part of the external genitalia, unrelated to the bladder's internal anatomy.

## title
Functional unit of the kidney

## question
The functional unit of the kidney is the ……..

## subject
renal

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
C

## answer_a
Loop of Henle

## explanation_a
Incorrect. The loop of Henle is one tubule segment within a nephron, not the whole functional unit.

## answer_b
Renal pelvis

## explanation_b
Incorrect. The renal pelvis is a collecting structure downstream of the nephrons, not itself the functional unit.

## answer_c
Nephron

## explanation_c
Correct. Each kidney contains roughly a million nephrons, the basic structural and functional units responsible for forming urine. A nephron comprises a renal corpuscle (glomerulus and Bowman's capsule) plus a tubule system that reabsorbs and secretes before draining into a collecting duct. It is the whole nephron, not any single part of it, that performs the kidney's filtering function.

## answer_d
Proximal convoluted tubule

## explanation_d
Incorrect. The proximal convoluted tubule is only one segment of a nephron, not the entire functional unit.

## answer_e
Distal convoluted tubule

## explanation_e
Incorrect. The distal convoluted tubule is likewise only one segment of a nephron.

Structure surrounding the neck of the bladder

## question
The neck of the urinary bladder is surrounded by the ………

## subject
renal

## status

## owner

## vignette

## correct_answer
C

## answer_a
External urethral sphincter

## explanation_a
Incorrect. The external urethral sphincter is a skeletal-muscle sphincter surrounding the membranous urethra, not the bladder neck.

## answer_b
Voluntary sphincter

## explanation_b
Incorrect. "Voluntary sphincter" describes the external, not internal, urethral sphincter, which does not surround the bladder neck.

## answer_c
Internal urethral sphincter

## explanation_c
Correct. The most proximal part of the male urethra, the intramural (preprostatic) part at the bladder neck, is surrounded by the internal urethral sphincter, a smooth-muscle sphincter under autonomic control that prevents retrograde ejaculation and involuntary leakage. This is distinct from the external urethral sphincter, a skeletal-muscle sphincter under voluntary control that surrounds the membranous part further downstream. The bladder neck and the intramural/preprostatic urethra describe the same anatomical region from two different reference points.

## answer_d
Skeletal muscle

## explanation_d
Incorrect. Skeletal muscle describes the external, not internal, urethral sphincter.

## answer_e
Cardiac muscle

## explanation_e
Incorrect. Cardiac muscle is found only in the heart, never in the urinary tract.

## title
Longest part of the male urethra

## question
The longest part of male urethra is…….

## subject
renal

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
C

## answer_a
Membranous

## explanation_a
Incorrect. The membranous part, passing through the urogenital diaphragm, is actually the narrowest and one of the shortest parts of the male urethra.

## answer_b
Prostatic

## explanation_b
Incorrect. The prostatic part, passing through the prostate gland, is longer than the membranous part but shorter than the penile part.

## answer_c
Penile

## explanation_c
Correct. The male urethra has four named parts — preprostatic (intramural), prostatic, membranous and spongy (penile) — and the penile (spongy) part, running the length of the penis, is the longest of the four. This is the same four-part classification under which the membranous part is separately known as the narrowest segment. The penile part's length reflects the distance it must travel along the shaft of the penis to the external urethral orifice.

## answer_d
Neck

## explanation_d
Incorrect. "Neck" refers to the bladder neck region, upstream of the urethra proper, not one of its four named parts.

## answer_e
Navicular fossa

## explanation_e
Incorrect. The navicular fossa is a small dilated segment near the tip of the penile urethra, not the longest part overall.

## title
Formation of the renal pelvis

## question
The renal pelvis is formed by the union of……….

## subject
renal

## status
Draft

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
A

## answer_a
Major calyces.

## explanation_a
Correct. Urine collects from the collecting ducts into the minor calyces, which unite into the major calyces, which in turn unite to form the funnel-shaped renal pelvis. The renal pelvis is therefore formed directly by the union of the major calyces, the step immediately preceding it in this collecting chain. The renal pelvis is continuous inferiorly with the ureter.

## answer_b
Minor calyces.

## explanation_b
Incorrect. The minor calyces unite to form the major calyces first; they do not unite directly to form the renal pelvis.

## answer_c
Collecting tubules

## explanation_c
Incorrect. Collecting tubules drain into the minor calyces, an earlier step in the chain, not directly into the renal pelvis.

## answer_d
Renal pyramids.

## explanation_d
Incorrect. Renal pyramids are the tissue structures whose papillae project into the minor calyces, not the structures whose union forms the renal pelvis.

## answer_e
Nephrons.

## explanation_e
Incorrect. Nephrons are the functional units producing filtrate; several steps of the collecting system lie between them and the renal pelvis.

The part of the urinary bladder which receives the opening of ureters is the ……

## subject

Trigone

Correct. The trigone's three corners are marked by the two ureteric orifices, where the ureters enter the bladder obliquely, and the single internal urethral orifice below. Superior surface

Termination of the female urethra

## question
Where is the termination of the female urethra?

## subject
renal

## status

## owner
Claude

## vignette

## correct_answer
B

## answer_a
At the vestibule behind the vagina

## explanation_a
Incorrect. The female urethra's external opening lies anterior to (in front of), not behind, the vaginal opening.

## answer_b
At the vestibule in front of the vagina

## explanation_b
Correct. The female urethra opens at the external urethral orifice in the vestibule of the vagina, the region between the labia minora, positioned anterior to (in front of) the vaginal opening. This anterior relationship to the vagina is the standard landmark for locating the external urethral meatus on examination. It follows the urethra's short course from the bladder neck through the pelvic floor to this external opening.

Incorrect. The urethra opens at the vestibule, external to the vagina, not within the vagina's own anterior wall.

In the neck of the urinary bladder

## explanation_d
Incorrect. The neck of the urinary bladder is where the urethra begins internally, not its external termination point.

## answer_e
In the trigon of the urinary bladder

## explanation_e
Incorrect. The trigone is an internal bladder landmark at the urethra's origin, not the site of its external termination.
