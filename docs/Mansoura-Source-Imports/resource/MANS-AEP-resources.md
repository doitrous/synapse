A 254-page compiled question bank distributed to the AEP (Anatomy/Embryology/Physiology) cohort, gathering multiple past exam sittings organised first by lecture (Anatomy Lectures 1-N, then Embryology, then Physiology) and later by whole combined final-exam sittings (e.g. "Final Exam 58 دفعة"). This lane's first cluster authored from the Anatomy Lecture 1 ("Terms"/"Anatomy Terminology") and Lecture 2 ("Bones"/"Skeletal system") sections only (p.4-13); the remaining ~240 pages (further Anatomy lectures, Embryology, Physiology, and the combined-sitting finals) are catalogued but not yet triaged. The AEP Telegram cohort label spans two S1 module codes, `MANS-PAEHC` and `MANS-PPP` (unresolved split per the lane card); this resource, and the `MANS-AEP` module id used for this cluster's questions, is filed under the Telegram label pending that split's resolution.
