## description
A 254-page compiled question bank distributed to the AEP (Anatomy/Embryology/Physiology) cohort, gathering multiple past exam sittings organised first by lecture (Anatomy Lectures 1-N, then Embryology, then Physiology) and later by whole combined final-exam sittings (e.g. "Final Exam 58 دفعة"). Every sampled question carries a printed single-letter answer in a right-margin column beside the stem — not a highlight or bold mark — recovered by plain text extraction across the whole document (p.4-13, 17-21, 23-29 sampled in depth for Anatomy Lectures 1-5; p.98-102 sampled in depth for Embryology Lecture 5; p.250-252 spot-checked for a combined Physiology sitting). Three clusters are authored so far: the Anatomy Lecture 1 ("Terms"/"Anatomy Terminology") and Lecture 2 ("Bones"/"Skeletal system") sections (p.4-13, lane 1); the Anatomy Lecture 3 ("Joints") and Embryology Lecture 5 ("2nd Week of Development") sections (p.17-21, 98-102, lane 2) — p.14-15 is a third "Bones" exam sitting restating Lecture 2 facts already authored by lane 1 and was held rather than reopened; and the Anatomy Lectures 4-5 "CNS"/"Nervous System" section (p.23-29, three compiled sittings, lane 3). Lane 3 confirmed by reading the full p.23-77 range that CNS/Nervous-System content is confined to p.23-29 (7 pages) — p.30 onward is a new lecture, Anatomy Lecture 6 "GIT" (starting p.31), followed by further unrelated lectures (Urinary p.52, Lymphatic p.57, Scalp & Face p.68-77); the dispatch's p.23-77 page-range estimate for this section was too wide, and this correction is logged here for whichever session next tackles this module. The remaining pages (Embryology beyond Lecture 5, Physiology, Lecture 6 "GIT" onward, and the combined-sitting finals) are catalogued but not yet triaged. The AEP Telegram cohort label spans two S1 module codes, `MANS-PAEHC` and `MANS-PPP` (unresolved split per the lane card); this resource, and the `MANS-AEP` module id used for this cluster's questions, is filed under the Telegram label pending that split's resolution.
