# Item

## label
Good communication does not by itself reduce the number of questions patients ask

## id
CON-PSY-90215449B9F8B0

## canonical_key
communication.benefits.does-not-reduce-patient-questions

## aliases
Benefits of good communication
Effective communication outcomes

## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
Good doctor-patient communication improves diagnostic accuracy, patient compliance and satisfaction, and reduces complaints against physicians. It does not reduce the number of questions patients ask; an engaged, well-informed patient is expected to ask more questions, not fewer.

## explicit_objective
Distinguish the true benefits of good communication (diagnosis, compliance, satisfaction, fewer complaints, faster recovery) from the false one (fewer patient questions).

## pitfalls
Assuming that a "good" outcome (fewer questions) must be a benefit of good communication, when in fact good communication invites more patient questions, not fewer.
