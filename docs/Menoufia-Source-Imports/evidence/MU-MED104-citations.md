## support_span
Why vit D is considered a hormone?
(1) Structurally both have "cyclopentanoperhydrophenanthrene" nucleus like a steroid hormone.
(2) Vitamin D3 (cholecalciferol) is synthesized in human skin by UV irradiation from its precursor Provitamin D3 (7-dehydrocholesterol).
(4) Like hormones, the formation of both the biological active forms 25-OH-D3 and 1,25-di-OH-D3 are subject to "feedback" inhibition.
(5) Like hormones, calcitriol has definite "target" organs like small intestine, bones and kidneys to act upon.
(6) Calcitriol resembles steroid hormone in its mode of action, i.e., nuclear action.
