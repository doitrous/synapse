## Source preference and handling

- Priority was given to Menoufia University and Faculty/Department pages and files under `mu.menofia.edu.eg` or `menofia.edu.eg`.
- Officially linked Google Drive documents were used only where the Menoufia page linked to them.
- No Telegram, Desktop files, Synapse import, commits, pushes, or video/audio downloads were used.
