## definition
An umbrella term for impairments, activity limitations and participation restrictions — and, in WHO's current framing, something arising from the interaction between a health condition and the world around it rather than from the condition alone.
## explicit_objective
Given a patient with a health condition, name one environmental factor that would change how disabling it is.
## pitfalls
Locating disability in the body alone. Both cited definitions place it partly outside the person: one in participation, the other explicitly in environmental factors.

## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
The whole of what a person does — body functions, activities and participation — of which activity is executing a task and participation is involvement in a life situation.
## explicit_objective
Given a patient who can perform a task in clinic but does not do it at home, say which ICF component has the problem.
## pitfalls
Collapsing activity into participation. A person can be able to do something and still not take part, and the two are separated precisely because interventions for them differ.
