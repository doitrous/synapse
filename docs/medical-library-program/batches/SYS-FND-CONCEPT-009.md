# Item
## label
Drug distribution
## id
med.concept.drug-distribution
## canonical_key
drug-distribution
## aliases
Distribution | Tissue distribution
## arabic_label
توزع الدواء
## arabic_aliases
انتشار الدواء
## definition
The movement of unchanged drug from the blood into the tissues, which is uneven because perfusion, tissue binding, pH and membrane permeability all differ from one tissue to the next.
## explicit_objective
Given two tissues, say which will see more drug and why.
## pitfalls
Assuming a drug reaches all tissues equally. Distribution is uneven by default, and the unevenness is what makes some sites hard to treat.
