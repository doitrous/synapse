## definition
The four behaviours that drive most chronic disease — diet, physical activity, tobacco and alcohol — each modifiable, and each acting over decades rather than days.
## explicit_objective
Name the four behavioural risk factors and say which chronic diseases they converge on.
## pitfalls
Treating lifestyle as a matter of individual choice alone. Diet and activity are shaped by what is available and affordable, which is why these are addressed as population problems as well as personal ones.

Risk assessment
## id

risk-assessment
## aliases

## arabic_label
تقييم الخطورة
## arabic_aliases
تقدير المخاطر
## definition
Estimating a person's probability of a future event from all their risk factors together, so that preventive action is matched to how likely the event actually is.
## explicit_objective
Explain why two patients with the same blood pressure may warrant different action.
## pitfalls
Treating each risk factor separately. The decision rests on total risk, so a modest abnormality in someone with several factors can matter more than a marked one in someone with none.
