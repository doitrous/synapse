## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
The ordered sequence a cell passes through from its creation at the end of one division until it divides again, comprising interphase and mitosis.
## explicit_objective
Name the phases in order and state what the cell is doing in each, and say which checkpoint a given failure would be caught at.
## pitfalls
Treating interphase as a resting stage. It is where the cell grows and replicates its entire genome, and it occupies most of the cycle.

## arabic_label

## arabic_aliases

## definition
A membrane protein that binds an extracellular signal molecule and, on binding, generates intracellular signals that change how the cell behaves.
## explicit_objective
Explain why a signal that cannot cross the membrane can still change a cell's behaviour, and name the step that converts binding into an intracellular event.
## pitfalls
Assuming the receptor carries the signal molecule into the cell. It does not: binding changes the receptor, and the receptor generates a separate internal signal.

# Item
## label
Second messenger
## id
med.concept.second-messenger
## canonical_key
second-messenger
## aliases
Small intracellular mediator
## arabic_label
الرسول الثاني
## arabic_aliases
الوسيط داخل الخلوي
## definition
A small intracellular molecule generated in response to receptor activation, which carries and amplifies the signal inside the cell.
## explicit_objective
State why the extracellular signal is called the first messenger and the intracellular one the second, and explain how this arrangement amplifies a small signal.
## pitfalls
Treating amplification as a detail. One bound receptor can generate many second-messenger molecules, which is why a few hormone molecules can change a whole cell's behaviour.

## label
Apoptosis
## id
med.concept.apoptosis
## canonical_key
apoptosis
## aliases
Programmed cell death
## arabic_label
الاستماتة
## arabic_aliases
موت الخلية المبرمج
## definition
Programmed cell death: a controlled, energy-dependent dismantling of a cell by its own machinery, leaving the surrounding tissue undisturbed.
## explicit_objective
Distinguish apoptosis from necrosis by its trigger, its energy requirement and whether it provokes inflammation, and name the protease family that carries it out.
## pitfalls
Equating cell death with damage. Apoptosis is a normal and necessary process, and failure of it is as pathological as excess of it.
