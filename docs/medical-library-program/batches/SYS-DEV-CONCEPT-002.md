## definition
Acting before disease, or before its consequences: primary prevention removes risk in the healthy, tertiary prevention limits the damage of established disease, and immunisation is the clearest instance of the first.
## explicit_objective
Place a given intervention at the right level of prevention and say what it is acting on.
## pitfalls
Reading prevention as advice. Immunisation, screening and environmental change are all prevention, and none of them is a conversation.
